Why exhibit in Phuket in January

Phuket is the world's fifth-largest market for branded residences, with around US$14 billion of new supply in development.1 Twenty-three condominium projects launched on the island in 2025,2 and sixteen villa projects launched in the first half of 2026.3 The market is shaped by affluent international buyers, with an average property price of 12.8 million baht,4 and January is high season, when many of them are on the island.

Do I need a ticket for the property expo?

Your Thailand Boat Festival registration covers the expo. Early Bird registration is complimentary until 30 November 2026.

Who will be exhibiting?

Developers of luxury villas, branded residences and condominiums, property agencies, and legal and finance advisers for foreign buyers. The exhibitor list will be announced closer to the event.

Can foreigners buy property in Phuket?

Foreign buyers can own a condominium unit in their own name, within the foreign quota of each building. Foreigners cannot own land in their own name, so villas are usually held on a registered lease.

This is general information, not legal advice. Talk to an independent lawyer before you commit. Legal advisers will be in the hall.
