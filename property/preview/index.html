See the yachts, then see the homes

Thailand Boat Festival fills Boat Lagoon Marina with yachts for four afternoons in January. The expo takes the White House, the air-conditioned hall beside the docks. Walk the pontoons, then meet the developers behind Phuket's villas and residences, in the same evening and the same place.

  • DevelopersLuxury villas, branded residences and condominiums, presented by the companies building them
  • AgenciesResale villas and residences, and long-term leases, from agents who know each coast of the island
  • Advisers for foreign buyersOwnership, contracts and bringing funds in from abroad, answered in the hall
  • Lounge and Property TalksA seated lounge with refreshments, and short talks each day on buying in Phuket. Programme to be announced.

Where Phuket's buyers already are

Festival visitors come to a marina to look at yachts, in January, when international buyers are on the island. That is the audience luxury property is sold to, and the expo puts your project in the same building, a few steps from the docks.

Phuket is the world's fifth-largest market for branded residences, with around US$14 billion of new supply in development.1 Twenty-three condominium projects launched on the island in 20252 and sixteen villa projects in the first half of 2026.3 The average property price is 12.8 million baht.4

A small hall, by design

32 booth modules of 3 × 3 m along three walls, around a shared lounge and a Property Talks stage. Book one module, or combine several for a larger space.

Registration and concierge sit at the entrance and the lounge sits at the far end, so every visitor passes every booth on the way to a seat.
