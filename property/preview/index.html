See the yachts, then see the homes

Thailand Boat Festival fills Boat Lagoon Marina with yachts for four days in January. The expo takes the White House, the air-conditioned hall beside the docks. Walk the pontoons, then meet the developers behind Phuket's villas and residences, the same afternoon and in the same place.

Where Phuket's buyers already are

Festival visitors come to a marina to look at yachts, in January, when international buyers are on the island. That is the audience luxury property is sold to, and the expo puts your project in the same building, a few steps from the docks.

52 booths, one loop

52 booth modules of 3 × 3 m: 26 along the walls and 26 on a centre island. Book one module, or combine two for a 6 × 3 m booth.

Registration sits under the canopy outside, so the whole hall is booths. Aisles are 3.5 m wide and one loop passes every booth. The four island ends face the entrance and the rear aisle.

Market figures and sources

Phuket is the world's fifth-largest market for branded residences, with around US$14 billion of new supply in development.1 Twenty-three condominium projects launched on the island in 20252 and sixteen villa projects in the first half of 2026.3 The average property price is 12.8 million baht.4

Do I need a ticket for the property expo?

No. Entry is by registration, and one registration covers both events, whether you register through Phuket Property Expo or Thailand Boat Festival. Early Bird registration closes on 30 November 2026.

Who will be exhibiting?

Developers of luxury villas, branded residences and condominiums, presenting their own projects, together with property agencies and advisers for foreign buyers. The exhibitor list will be announced closer to the event.
