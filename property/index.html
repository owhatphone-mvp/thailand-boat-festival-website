See the yacht.
Then see the home.

Phuket Property Expo takes over the White House, the air-conditioned hall within the Thailand Boat Festival grounds. Walk the docks in the afternoon, then meet developers, agencies and property advisers in the same venue, on the same day.

01

Luxury property, in one hall

32 booths for developers, branded residences and agencies, with legal and finance advisers for foreign buyers.

02

Documents first

Each property project in the hall is asked to show its title deed, building permits and ownership structure for foreign buyers.

03

Shared lounge and daily talks

A lounge in the centre of the hall with seating and refreshments, and Property Talks every day. Programme to be announced.
