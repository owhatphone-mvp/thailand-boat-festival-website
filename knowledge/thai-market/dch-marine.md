## Identity

DCH Marine is an Asia-Pacific luxury yacht distributor led by **Adam Waters**, headquartered in Hong Kong. Part of the broader DCH Group (associated with CITIC / Dah Chong Hong, a major HK trading conglomerate). DCH Marine serves as the exclusive distributor for several premium yacht and marine brands across the region, with a growing footprint from Hong Kong to Australia. Per dch-marine.com, Yacht Style, group.citic.

## Brands Distributed

- **Sunseeker** — exclusive distributor for all of Asia-Pacific (appointed by Sunseeker International). The headline brand.
- **Saxdor** — exclusive APAC distributor (appointed February 2024)
- **Williams Jet Tenders** — luxury yacht tenders

DCH Marine is one of the most significant new entrants in the Asian yacht distribution landscape, rapidly assembling a premium multi-brand portfolio. Per sunseeker.com, saxdoryachts.com, dch-marine.com.

## Regional Coverage

Offices in Hong Kong (HQ), Shenzhen, Singapore, Phuket (Thailand — Royal Phuket Marina), and Australia (Gold Coast, Sydney, Western Australia). In Thailand, DCH Marine operates from **Royal Phuket Marina**, the same marina where Sunseeker was shown at TBF 2026. Per dch-marine.com, Yacht Style.

## TBF Context

DCH Marine exhibited **Sunseeker** at TBF 2026 (90 Ocean and Manhattan 55), one of the headline displays. Saxdor was shown at the same event by Motor Field (not DCH Marine), suggesting the two brands may have different retail arrangements in Thailand despite DCH Marine being the regional distributor. Per sunseeker.com events, Yacht Style.
