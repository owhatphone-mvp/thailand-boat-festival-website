## Identity

DCH Marine is a Hong Kong-headquartered yacht distributor owned by the **Dah Chong Hong (DCH)** group. It was founded in 2013 as Princess Yachts Greater China and renamed DCH Marine after ending the Princess dealership (it still supports existing Princess owners with warranty/service). Managing Director (per 2024–25 press): **Adam Waters**.

## Brands Represented (as of 2026)

- **Sunseeker** — exclusive master distributor for Asia-Pacific; the headline brand
- **Saxdor** — Finnish day boats; appointed APAC distributor in 2024
- **Williams Jet Tenders**

Saxdor in Thailand: before DCH's APAC deal, Saxdor's Thai dealer was Motor Field Thailand (from 2021). The current Thai retail arrangement is unclear — verify with DCH Marine.

## Where They Are

- **Thailand: Royal Phuket Marina** (east coast of Phuket) — office opened 14 Jan 2026; sales, after-sales and technical support
- Hong Kong (HQ), Shenzhen, Singapore (since June 2024)
- Australia: Sydney, Sanctuary Cove (Gold Coast), Perth; partner network incl. New Zealand

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)

- Jan 2026: Royal Phuket Marina office opened, just before the last edition
- Mar 2026: Hong Kong showcase — Asia premieres of the Sunseeker 100 Yacht and 82 Ocean Enclosed, plus Manhattan 55 and Saxdor 270 GTO / 320 GTO / 400 GTC
- 2025: added Thailand country management and expanded in Australia
