## Identity
Thailand is one of Asia's more open places to own and base a yacht. Recreational boats have been exempt from import duty since 2004, and VAT is currently 7%. Foreign-flagged yachts can stay up to about 2.5 years, and those of 24m+ can charter under licence. All of this is general guidance, not legal advice.

## Quick Facts (as of Oct 2026 — verify before acting)
- **Import duty:** 0% on recreational vessels, under a Customs exemption in force since Feb 2004 (also excise). Commercial, fishing and passenger vessels are classified differently.
- **VAT:** 7% on the CIF value at import. The legal rate is 10%; the 7% rate was extended by Cabinet (Jul 2026) to **30 Sep 2027**.
- **Foreign yachts visiting:** temporary import up to ~2.5 years (6 months plus extensions, since 2021).
- **Charter by foreign-flagged superyachts:** allowed for yachts of **24m+** (lowered from 30m in Sep 2024), max 12 guests, licence required.

## Import Duty & VAT
- Example: a yacht with a CIF value of USD 1M pays about USD 70K in VAT, plus fees and agent costs.
- A customs broker should confirm the tariff classification before shipping.

## Registration Options
**Thai flag:**
- Generally needs majority Thai ownership (commonly cited at 70% Thai) — usually via a Thai company.
- This makes domestic navigation and clearance simpler, but the company structure needs a Thai maritime lawyer.

**Offshore flags (common for Asian owners):**
- Marshall Islands, Cayman Islands and BVI are popular. Offshore-flagged yachts use temporary import arrangements in Thai waters.

## Foreign Charter Licence
- Applies to foreign-flagged yachts of **24m+** carrying a maximum of 12 passengers.
- Insurance cover of at least **USD 5M per incident** is required.
- Applications are made online (Thai or English documents). The target decision time is 25 working days.
- The licence has historically been valid for 12 months. VAT is payable on charter income earned in Thailand.

## Key Considerations
- Foreign crew need appropriate visas and work permits.
- Marina fees are unregulated and vary by marina and season.
