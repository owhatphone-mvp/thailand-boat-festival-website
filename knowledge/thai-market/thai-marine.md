## Identity

"Thai Marine" is an ambiguous name in Thai yachting. As of Oct 2026, no prominent yacht dealership trading as "Thai Marine" was found. A user saying "Thai Marine" may mean one of these:

- **Thai Marine Guide** (thaimarineguide.com) — an online directory of Thailand's yachting, boating and leisure-marine businesses. A directory, not a dealer.
- **Thai Yachting Business Association (TYBA)** — the trade body for Thailand's yacht businesses. It promotes the sector and has lobbied on regulation, including the superyacht charter licence (yachts over 30m), which lets foreign-flagged yachts stay longer in Thai waters.
- **AusThai Marine** (austhaimarine.com) — a Thailand-based builder of catamarans and dive/work boats.
- Generic phrasing for "the Thai marine industry" or a small local company.

## Related Names That Are Not "Thai Marine"

- **Boat Lagoon Yachting** — Princess, Jeanneau and SACS dealer
