## Identity

Simpson Marine is one of Asia-Pacific's largest yacht dealers and service companies, founded in Hong Kong in 1984 by **Mike Simpson**. Since **March 2024 it is 95% owned by Sanlorenzo** (Italy), and serves as Sanlorenzo's APAC sales and service network.

## Brands Represented (per its website, as of Oct 2026)

- **Sanlorenzo** and **Bluegame** (Sanlorenzo's sport-yacht brand)
- **Nautor Swan** — APAC agent since April 2024
- **Axopar** — Hong Kong and China (since 2024); in Thailand Axopar is handled by another dealer
- **Highfield** tenders, **Fairline** (still listed; markets vary), **Seabob**

**In Thailand, new Sanlorenzo and Bluegame yachts are sold through Sanlorenzo Thailand** — a dedicated company launched Aug 2025 as a partnership between Sanlorenzo APAC and Thai yachting entrepreneur Vrit Yongsakul. Simpson Marine handles brokerage, charter and after-sales (its Phuket base is the regional SE Asia hub for Sanlorenzo after-sales).

**No longer represented:** Azimut (ended 2015) and Princess (Hong Kong, ended 2024).

## Where They Are

- **Thailand: Ao Po Grand Marina, Phuket** (Simpson Marine — brokerage, service, charter)
- **Sanlorenzo Thailand: Phuket Boat Lagoon Marina** (new-yacht sales)
- Regional offices: Hong Kong (HQ), Singapore, Shenzhen, Sanya, Taipei, Kaohsiung, Jakarta, Port Dickson, Ho Chi Minh City, Sydney, Yokohama
