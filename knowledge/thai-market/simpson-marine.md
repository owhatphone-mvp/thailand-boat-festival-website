## Identity

Simpson Marine is Asia-Pacific's largest multi-brand yacht dealership, founded in 1984 by **Mike Simpson** in Hong Kong. The company operates 11 offices across 10+ strategic locations in Asia-Pacific and has been a dominant force in the region's yacht industry for over 40 years. Per simpsonmarine.com, Yacht Style, BODW.

## Brands Distributed (current)

- **Sanlorenzo** — exclusive APAC dealer since the brand's Asian expansion, with the 2024 Thailand partnership via Vrit Yongsakul/Boat Lagoon Yachting
- **Bluegame** — Sanlorenzo's sport-yacht sub-brand
- **Fairline** — British luxury motor yachts
- **Axopar** — exclusive dealer for Hong Kong and China (appointed August 2024)

**Historical brands no longer represented:**
- **Azimut** — Simpson parted ways with Azimut in **May 2015** after a 28-year relationship. Azimut is now handled by MGC Marine in Thailand.
- **Princess** — previously represented in HK; replaced by Sunazure NextWave (September 2024).

Per simpsonmarine.com, Yacht Style, azimutyachts.com, princesshongkong.com.

## Regional Coverage

11 offices: Hong Kong (HQ), Singapore, Phuket (Thailand), Sydney (Australia), Sanya, Shenzhen (China), Jakarta (Indonesia), Yokosuka (Japan), Port Dickson (Malaysia), Kaohsiung (Taiwan), Ho Chi Minh City (Vietnam). Per simpsonmarine.com, apac.sanlorenzoyacht.com.

## TBF Context

Simpson Marine exhibited the **Sanlorenzo SL90A** at TIBS 2025 (Phuket Yacht Haven Marina). At TBF 2026, Sanlorenzo was exhibited through "Sanlorenzo Thailand" (the Vrit Yongsakul/BLY partnership operating via Simpson Marine's network). Per Yacht Style, Simpson Marine news.
