## Summary

Phuket's luxury real estate around its major marinas appeals strongly to yacht owners seeking a residence or investment base. Areas like Boat Lagoon, Cape Yamu (Ao Po), Cherng Talay, and Kamala's "Millionaire's Mile" range from pool villas to ultra-luxury oceanfront estates. Foreign buyers must navigate Thailand's land ownership restrictions, typically through leasehold structures or Thai company arrangements.

## Key Information

**Prime Areas Near Marinas**

Boat Lagoon is Phuket's most integrated marina-residential community. The Estate offers freehold condo units and leasehold villas within walking distance of berths. Per Siam Real Estate, 2026, properties range from apartments to six-bedroom pool villas.

Cape Yamu (Ao Po) on the east coast is adjacent to Ao Po Grand Marina. Villa prices range from approximately 80 to 300 million THB (roughly USD 2.2M to USD 8.5M+). Per Varsovia Estate, 2026, the area attracts buyers wanting sea views and marina proximity.

Cherng Talay and the Laguna area near Bang Tao Beach feature branded residences (Banyan Tree, Angsana). Pool villas typically list at 40-120 million THB. Per Varsovia Estate, 2026.

Kamala's "Millionaire's Mile" is Phuket's most prestigious stretch, with cliffside estates at 70 to 250+ million THB. Per Varsovia Estate, 2026, these offer dramatic views but are farther from east-coast marinas.

Natai Beach, north of Sarasin Bridge, is an emerging luxury corridor with larger land plots.

**Ballpark Pricing**

- Three-to-four-bedroom pool villa (standard luxury): 15-40 million THB (approximately USD 425,000 to USD 1.15M).
- High-end villa with sea views: 40-120 million THB (approximately USD 1.15M to USD 3.4M).
- Ultra-luxury estate (Kamala, Cape Yamu oceanfront): 120-300+ million THB (approximately USD 3.4M to USD 8.5M+).

**Foreign Ownership Rules**

Under Thai law, foreigners cannot own land directly but can own condominium units freehold if the foreign quota (49% of total unit area) is not exceeded. Per Savills, 2025, for villas the standard mechanism is a 30-year leasehold, often structured as 30+30+30 years (renewals are contractual, not legally guaranteed).

Thai company structures, where a Thai-majority company holds the land, are widely used but legally grey. Thai law prohibits nominee shareholders acting on behalf of foreigners. Per Phuket News Property, 2025, companies must demonstrate legitimate commercial activity. Buyers considering this route must engage qualified Thai legal counsel.

**Rental Yields**

Phuket villa rental yields typically range from 5-8% gross and 4-7% net after fees, maintenance, and taxes. Per MORE Group, 2026, condominiums in managed resort programmes can achieve 7-12% gross. Variables include location, management quality, and proximity to beach or marina.

## Asian / Thai Market Context

The post-pandemic demand surge has stabilised into steady growth. Russian, Chinese, and Australian buyers remain prominent, with increasing interest from Southeast Asian and Middle Eastern buyers. Marina-adjacent properties command a premium for their lifestyle convenience to yacht owners.

The Thai government has periodically discussed easing foreign ownership restrictions, including long-term resident visa proposals tied to property investment, but no major changes have been enacted as of mid-2026.

## Talking Points for Sand

- Boat Lagoon is the only Phuket marina with integrated residential and berthing; ideal for walk-to-boat convenience.
- Cape Yamu / Ao Po is best for superyacht owners needing deep-water marina access.
- Always recommend foreign buyers consult a qualified Thai property lawyer.
- Leasehold is the standard, legally straightforward route for villas; 30+30+30 renewal clauses should be carefully reviewed.
- Rental yields of 5-8% gross are realistic for well-managed villas.
