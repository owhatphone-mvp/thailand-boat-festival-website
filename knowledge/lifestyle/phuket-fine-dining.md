## Summary

Phuket's dining scene has matured considerably, anchored by PRU at Trisara (the island's sole Michelin-starred restaurant) and a growing roster of Bib Gourmand venues. For yacht owners, options extend beyond restaurants: private chef services for onboard dining, upscale beach clubs, and improving wine availability following Thailand's recent tax reductions contribute to a credible luxury food ecosystem.

## Key Information

**Michelin Guide Phuket 2026**

PRU, within Trisara resort in Cherng Talay, holds Phuket's only Michelin star. Per Michelin Guide Thailand 2026, PRU specialises in innovative Thai cuisine using locally sourced, sustainably farmed ingredients.

Blue Elephant Phuket, in a historic Sino-Portuguese governor's mansion, has retained Michelin recognition for multiple years. Per Blue Elephant, 2025, it serves Royal Thai cuisine and suits elegant private dinners.

Bib Gourmand restaurants include One Chun (Southern Thai), Mor Mu Dong, Go Benz, The Charm, and Chuan Chim. Per Phuket 101, 2026.

Michelin-selected restaurants relevant to yacht owners include Acqua (Italian, Wine Spectator Award winner), L'Arome (French contemporary), Tambu (modern Indian), and Suay by Chef Tammasak (contemporary Thai).

**Marina-Area Dining**

Boat Lagoon has casual-to-mid-range restaurants along the marina walkway, plus the Harbour Restaurant at NH Boat Lagoon. Per NH Hotels, 2025. Ao Po Grand Marina features an on-site restaurant. Per Ao Po Grand Marina, 2025.

For yacht owners, dining at a top restaurant one evening and arranging a private chef onboard the next is a practical approach.

**Beach Clubs**

Catch Beach Club (Bang Tao) offers daybeds, champagne service, and sunset sessions. Cafe del Mar Phuket (Kamala) programmes international DJs during November-May and draws the younger HNWI crowd. Per Cafe del Mar, 2025/26. Carpe Diem Beach Club on Ao Po offers a relaxed Mediterranean-style experience near the marina district.

**Private Chef Services**

Phuket Chef Service, J&J Private Chef Services, and The Legend Private Chef & Catering offer full-service onboard or in-villa dining, from casual BBQ to multi-course tasting menus. Per Phuket Dream Company, 2025, services include menu planning, sourcing, cooking, service staff, and cleanup.

**Wine and Alcohol**

Thailand reduced wine duties in 2024, cutting excise tax from 10% to 5% and removing the 10% surcharge. Per Acclime Thailand, 2025, customs duties of 54-60% were temporarily exempted, though these have expiration dates. Wine Connection has retail shops across Phuket. The Wine Merchant Thailand and Global Wines are specialist importers. Wine remains notably more expensive than in Singapore or Hong Kong.

## Asian / Thai Market Context

Phuket's fine dining competes favourably within Southeast Asia, though it lacks the density of Michelin-starred venues in Bangkok or Singapore. The island's strength is variety: world-class Thai, credible Italian and French, growing Indian and Japanese options, and wellness-focused dining at resorts like Amanpuri and Trisara.

For yacht owners hosting international guests, private chef services for casual onboard meals combined with restaurant reservations for special occasions provides appreciated flexibility.
