## Summary

Phuket has a growing ecosystem of luxury concierge and lifestyle management services catering to yacht owners and their guests. From yacht provisioning and event planning to luxury car hire and wellness retreats, these services bridge marina life and onshore experiences. Global operators like Quintessentially maintain a Thai presence alongside local specialists.

## Key Information

**Concierge Operators**

Quintessentially, the London-founded global lifestyle management company, operates in Thailand serving ultra-high-net-worth members. Per Luxury Lifestyle Awards, Quintessentially Thailand is a recognised leading service. Membership is typically invitation-only or fee-based.

Phuket Concierge is a local specialist offering villa management, yacht charter coordination, and lifestyle services. Per Phuket Concierge, 2025, services include airport transfers, event planning, restaurant bookings, and guest management.

Discovery DMC provides luxury travel concierge including airport fast-track, helicopter transfers, and bespoke itineraries. Per Discovery DMC, 2025. Marina-adjacent villa management companies also bundle property management with guest services.

**Yacht Provisioning**

Phuket Provisions and Just Provisions are specialist suppliers for the yachting community. Per Superyacht Services Guide, they handle fresh produce, premium meats, and brand-specific requests delivered to the berth. The Phuket Marine Guide is a directory for marine services including provisioning, engineering, and crew placement. Per Phuket Marine Guide, 2025.

Superyacht provisioning is well-established, with suppliers meeting detailed provision lists. Turnaround is typically 24-48 hours.

**Luxury Automotive**

Supercar rental is available through Prime Cars Rental Thailand, offering Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, and Porsche models. Per Prime Cars, 2026, daily rates are in the ballpark of several thousand USD. Chauffeured limousines including Mercedes S-Class and Toyota Alphard VIP vans are widely available. SIXT at Phuket Airport offers luxury self-drive options. Per SIXT Thailand, 2025.

**Wellness and Spas**

Phuket's wellness offerings rank among Thailand's finest. Banyan Tree Phuket's spa is a multi-award winner. Per Banyan Tree, 2025. Amanpuri's Wellness Centre blends ancient healing with modern therapies including the Banya experience. Per Aman, 2025. Trisara's spa offers private treatment villas with ocean views.

Medical tourism is significant: accredited hospitals (Bangkok Hospital Phuket, Siriroj International) offer health checks, dental, and elective procedures at a fraction of Western prices. Detox retreats are available at Amatara Wellness Resort.

**Shopping**

Central Phuket (including the Floresta wing) is the island's luxury shopping destination, housing Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and Prada. Per Central Phuket, 2025. Bespoke tailoring shops include Star Tailor House (since 1992) and Anthony's Bespoke Tailor, offering custom garments at Thai price points.

## Asian / Thai Market Context

Phuket's concierge industry has matured since the pandemic, driven by high-spending visitors and luxury developments. The island competes with Bali, Langkawi, and the Maldives, but its marina infrastructure, international airport, and diverse amenities give it an edge for yacht owners.

Phuket's luxury services are improving but remain a tier below Singapore or Hong Kong in consistency. Booking well in advance during peak season (December-February) is advisable.

## Talking Points for Sand

- Quintessentially is the global gold standard; Phuket Concierge is the local specialist.
- Yacht provisioning is well-organised; suppliers deliver to any major marina with 24-48 hours' notice.
- Supercar rental is available but limited during peak season; early booking is essential.
- Banyan Tree, Amanpuri, and Trisara spas are top recommendations for wellness.
- Central Phuket Floresta is the go-to for luxury shopping; bespoke tailoring offers excellent value.
- Sand can recommend concierge services but should not act as booking agent.

## DO NOT

- Book services on behalf of users; recommend operators and let users arrange.
- Quote exact prices for car rentals, spa treatments, or provisioning.
- Guarantee availability of any specific vehicle, service, or appointment.
- Recommend medical procedures; refer to accredited hospitals only.
- Name individual concierge staff by personal name.
