## Summary

Private aviation and yachting are deeply interconnected lifestyles for high-net-worth individuals. HondaJet was an exhibitor at TBF 2026, underscoring the natural synergy between light private jets and yacht ownership. For owners based in or visiting Phuket, private aviation offers rapid access from major Asian hubs, with charter and fractional ownership options increasingly available across the region.

## Key Information

**HondaJet at TBF 2026**

Honda Aircraft Company exhibited at Thailand Boat Festival 2026 at Phuket Boat Lagoon, highlighting the connection between owner-pilots and the yachting community. The HondaJet Elite II is a very light jet seating 4-5 passengers, with a range of approximately 1,223 nm and a cruise speed of 422 knots. Per Safe Fly Aviation, 2025, the HA-420 is priced around USD 5.2-5.3 million new. Its over-the-wing engine mount provides a notably spacious cabin for its class.

**Jet Categories and Typical Charter Costs**

For Southeast Asian routes, ballpark charter costs vary significantly by category:
- Light jets (HondaJet, Embraer Phenom 300): roughly USD 3,500-6,000 per flight hour.
- Midsize jets (Cessna Citation, Learjet 75): approximately USD 6,000-10,000 per flight hour.
- Large-cabin jets (Gulfstream G650, Bombardier Global): approximately USD 12,000-22,000+ per flight hour. Per Safe Fly Aviation, 2025.

A Bangkok-to-Phuket flight is roughly one hour by private jet versus 80 minutes commercially, but with no check-in queues, customs delays, or scheduling rigidity.

**Charter vs. Ownership in Asia**

Key regional operators include Asian Sky Group (ASG), one of Asia's largest aircraft brokerage and charter firms, which reported robust flight activity in Q4 2025 across Asia-Pacific. Per Asian Sky Group, 2025. Sino Jet holds the top Asia-Pacific business jet fleet and in 2025 launched its Unionsky Curated Collection, blending jet access with luxury lifestyle services. Per PR Newswire, 2025.

Fractional ownership and jet cards offer a middle ground, with programmes through VistaJet and NetJets primarily serving Hong Kong and Singapore corridors.

**Key Airports for Yacht Owners**

- Phuket International (HKT): approximately 30 minutes from Boat Lagoon Marina.
- Koh Samui (USM): Bangkok Airways monopoly; private jet access available via FBO.
- Langkawi (LGK): tax-free island, approximately 20 minutes from Rebak Marina.
- Singapore Seletar (XSP): dedicated business aviation terminal; key hub for regional jet owners.

## Asian / Thai Market Context

Thailand's business aviation sector has grown steadily since the pandemic, with Phuket a preferred destination for private jet arrivals during the November-April yachting season. However, Thailand lacks a dedicated FBO comparable to Singapore's Seletar Airport. Phuket International handles private jets through the general terminal, which is functional but lacks seamless privacy.

The HondaJet's presence at TBF 2026 signals growing interest in owner-pilot culture in Asia. In Europe and the US, many yacht owners also pilot their own aircraft; this crossover is nascent but growing in Southeast Asia.
