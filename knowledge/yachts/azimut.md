## Identity
Azimut Yachts is an Italian motor yacht builder headquartered in Avigliana (Turin region). Part of Azimut|Benetti Group — one of the largest privately-held yacht builders globally by volume. Best known for contemporary, refined flybridge and sport yachts in the 12–35m (40–115ft) range. Long collaboration with designer Stefano Righini for exterior lines and Achille Salvagni / Francesco Guida for interiors.

## Lineup (current major collections)

- **S Collection (Sport)** — sport coupé planing, hardchine hulls, IPS pod drive. Models S6, S7, S8.
- **Flybridge Collection** — twin-deck flybridge cruisers, the heart of the brand. From the Flybridge 50 up through 78, 88.
- **Magellano Collection** — semi-displacement, long-range, dual-mode hull. Models 25 Metri, 30 Metri.
- **Grande Collection** — superyacht tier above 75ft, semi-custom interiors. 25 Metri, 26 Metri, 27 Metri, 30 Metri, 32 Metri, 36 Metri.
- **Verve / Atlantis (sport)** — entry-level open cruisers, retired in recent years.

## Asian Market Position
Strong presence across Asia. Distributors vary by country — Singapore via Simpson Marine and Boat Lagoon Yachting; Hong Kong via Lantau Yachts / Simpson Marine; Greater China via dedicated Azimut China dealer network. Thailand handled by Simpson Marine and Boat Lagoon Yachting (verify).

## Positioning vs Competitors
Azimut occupies the "contemporary refined" middle of the Italian motor yacht market. Cleaner, less aggressive than Pershing. More accessible price point than Sanlorenzo's semi-custom approach. Broader lineup than Riva (which is heritage-prestige). Closer competitor to Princess (UK) at similar price tiers, though with Italian rather than British design language.
