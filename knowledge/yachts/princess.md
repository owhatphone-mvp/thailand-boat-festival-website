## Identity
British luxury motor yacht builder, founded 1965 in Plymouth, Devon, where it still builds its yachts. Owned by US investor KPS Capital Partners since 2023; CEO Will Green. Known for comfortable, high-quality flybridge, sportbridge and tri-deck yachts from ~15m to ~29m.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer
- **Boat Lagoon Yachting (BLY)**, based in Phuket, has represented Princess in Southeast Asia since 1994 — Asia's longest-standing Princess dealer — with Thailand and Singapore as key markets (as of 2026).
- Hong Kong and other Asian markets have separate dealers; verify on princessyachts.com.

## Lineup (current models)
- **V Class** (open/sport) — ~15–20m / 50–65ft. Ballpark USD 1–3M.
- **F Class** (flybridge) — ~15–20m / 50–65ft. Ballpark USD 1.2–3.5M.
- **S Class** (sportbridge) — S62 to S80 (~19–25m / 62–80ft). Ballpark USD 2.5–6.5M.
- **X Class** (super flybridge) — X80, **X90** (27m / 89ft), X95 Vista (~29m / 95ft). Ballpark USD 7–13M+.
- **Y Class** (motor yacht) — ~22–29m / 72–95ft. Ballpark USD 4.5–13M.
- **Coming:** C48 (14.6m / 48ft, first outboard model), F54 (three-cabin flybridge), S74 (new sportbridge), 106 Odyssey (~32m / 105ft, first launch planned 2028).

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)
- **Sep 2026 (Cannes):** world premiere of the **X90** (Super Flybridge); seven sold before launch.
- **Upcoming:** **C48** global debut at Fort Lauderdale (FLIBS, late Oct 2026) — expected to be the fastest Princess ever; **F54** debut at boot Düsseldorf (Jan 2027).
- **Jun 2026:** Princess said it returned to profitability ahead of plan, with 2025 EBITDA up over 100%.
- **Nov 2025:** **106 Odyssey** announced — fast-displacement hull, twin MAN V12s, ~2,500nm range at 10 knots; marks Princess's return above 30m.

## Positioning vs Competitors
"Sensible luxury" with British build quality and strong resale: more comfort-focused than Sunseeker, more classic than Azimut, more production-based and accessible than Sanlorenzo.
