## Identity

Princess Yachts is the UK's largest luxury yacht builder, founded 1965 in Plymouth, Devon, where it still manufactures with over 2,000 craftspeople. Owned since Q1 2023 by **KPS Capital Partners** (US PE firm, GBP 54M investment), with CEO Will Green leading a successful turnaround. 60th anniversary in 2025, over 20,000 yachts built. Tagline "Distinction in Detail" (2024) emphasises 80%+ in-house craftsmanship. Per princessyachts.com, kpsfund.com, Boat International.

## Lineup (current models, 2024-2026)

- **C-Class (NEW 2025)** — centre-console outboard, first of its kind for Princess. C48 Open and C48 Deck Saloon (14.6m/48ft). 50+ knots with triple Mercury. Ballpark USD 635K–760K.
- **V-Class** — sport cruiser / open. V40 to V65 (12–20m / 40–66ft). Ballpark USD 635K–2.5M.
- **F-Class** — flybridge cruisers. F45 to F65 (13.7–19.8m / 45–65ft). Ballpark USD 1.1M–3.5M.
- **S-Class** — sportbridge. S62 to S80 (18.9–25.4m / 62–83ft). Ballpark USD 2.5M–6.3M.
- **X-Class** — super flybridge. X80, X90 (new 2025), X95 (24.4–29m / 80–95ft). Ballpark USD 7.6M–12.7M+.
- **Y-Class** — tri-deck motor yacht. Y72 to Y95 (22–29.1m / 72–95ft). Ballpark USD 4.4M–12.7M+.
- **Odyssey 106 (announced Oct 2025)** — semi-custom superyacht, 32m+/106ft, hybrid fast displacement hull. Delivery ~2028. Marks Princess's return above 100ft.

The X90 (27m/89ft, debut Cannes Sept 2025) requires 10% less power above 16 knots than the smaller Y85 — a notable efficiency gain. Per princessyachts.com, YachtBuyer, Boat International, 2025-2026.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

The financial turnaround defines the story: GBP 45.6M loss (2023) to GBP 4.9M profit on GBP 378M revenue (2025). Cannes 2025 was "transformational" — eight models including C48 premiere and X90 debut. Sustainability: factory carbon cut 19.78% in 2025 via solar and biomass. Odyssey 106 hybrid (2028) will be their first hybrid system; no pure-electric announced. Per yachtbuyer.com, British Marine, 2025-2026.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**BLY** is Princess's exclusive distributor for Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, and Maldives — ~30 years (since 1994), Asia's longest-serving Princess dealer. HQ at Boat Lagoon Marina, Phuket, founded by Vrit Yongsakul. In Hong Kong, **Sunazure NextWave** was appointed September 2024. Per boatlagoonyachting.com, Yacht Style.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Princess occupies the "sensible luxury" sweet spot: British heritage, strong build quality, excellent resale values, and relatively accessible pricing within the luxury segment. Against Sunseeker (also UK), Princess trades outright speed for greater comfort and interior volume per foot. Against Azimut, Princess offers more conservative but timeless British design versus Italian boldness. Against Sanlorenzo, Princess is more production-oriented and accessible in price — typically 20-40% less per foot — while sacrificing some exclusivity and bespoke customization.
