Azimut acquired Benetti in 1985. This file is included because Benetti is highly relevant to the TBF audience as one of the world's top superyacht builders with growing Asian presence.

## Identity

Benetti is one of the world's oldest and most prestigious superyacht builders, founded in **1873** in Viareggio, Tuscany, by Lorenzo Benetti — a marble craftsman turned boatbuilder. Over 150 years and 300+ yachts delivered. Part of **Azimut|Benetti Group**, independently owned by the Vitelli family since 1985. The Group tops the global order book for 26 consecutive years and forecasts EUR 1.5B revenue for 2024/2025. Per benettiyachts.com, Boat International, SuperYacht Times.

## Lineup (current models, 2024-2026)

- **Class 44M** — 44.1m/144ft. Steel semi-custom. First unit "Fortitude" launched 2024. Hull 12 sold for 2027 delivery. Ballpark USD 25-30M.
- **B.Now 50M** — 49.9m. Steel hull, Oasis Deck option. 14+ hulls. Ballpark USD 30-40M.
- **B.Now 67M** — 66.2m, 1,326GT. First unit "Iryna" delivered April 2025. Ballpark USD 45-55M.
- **B.Custom** — full custom 50-100m+. Steel/aluminium. Ballpark USD 40-100M+.
- **CLine** — composite (fiberglass) semi-custom. Lower-cost customisation.
- **Oasis 40M** — breakout model. 40m, beach club, 12+ sold. Singapore delivery confirmed. Ballpark USD 20-28M.

Per benettiyachts.com, YachtBuyer, Boat International, Yacht Style, 2024-2026.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

The **B.Now 67M** "Iryna" was delivered April 2025 — 66.2m, 1,326GT, for a repeat client. The **Class 44M** continues selling (hull 12 confirmed for 2027). The **Oasis 40M** has climbed to 12+ sold units, with Singapore delivery confirmed. Azimut|Benetti reported a EUR 2.6B order book. At Monaco Yacht Show 2024, Benetti displayed two 67m custom yachts alongside a B.Now 50M. Per Boat International, SuperYacht Times, Yacht Style, 2024-2026.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

Benetti's Asia-Pacific sales go through the **Azimut|Benetti dealer network** — Azimut Yachts HK and **Marine Italia Asia** have facilitated Oasis 40M sales into Singapore. **Simpson Marine** (HK, 11 offices) has historically represented Azimut|Benetti brands regionally. No confirmed Thai Benetti delivery to date. Per Yacht Style, Asia Pacific Boating, 2023-2026.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Benetti sits at the apex of production superyacht building. Competitors include **Lurssen** (Germany, 80m+), **Feadship** (Netherlands, fully custom), **Sanlorenzo** (Italy, similar range), and **Amels/Damen** (Netherlands). Within Ferretti Group, **Custom Line** competes at the lower end (30-40m) but Benetti dominates above 50m. The Oasis 40M's beach club concept has been widely imitated.
