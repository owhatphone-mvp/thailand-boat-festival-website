## Identity
Benetti is an Italian superyacht builder founded in 1873 in Viareggio, Tuscany, with yards in Viareggio and Livorno. It belongs to the **Azimut|Benetti Group** (Vitelli family-owned since 1985) — NOT Ferretti Group. The Group topped the Global Order Book for the 26th year running (Yacht Style, Dec 2025).

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer
- Benetti sells superyachts through its own Asia sales team: Angel Zhou was appointed President of Sales for Asia from 15 Oct 2025.
- Past Asian sales also came via Azimut partners (e.g. Marine Italia for a Singapore Oasis 40M).
- No dedicated Thai Benetti dealer is confirmed. Sister brand Azimut is sold in Thailand by MGC Marine (MGC-ASIA), but that does not make MGC a Benetti dealer. Check with the yard.

## Positioning vs Competitors
Benetti covers the widest range among Italian superyacht yards, from 36m semi-custom to 100m+ custom. Peers include Sanlorenzo, Lürssen, Feadship, Amels and Heesen.
