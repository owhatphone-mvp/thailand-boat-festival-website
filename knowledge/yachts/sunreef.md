## Identity

Sunreef Yachts is a Polish builder of luxury semi-custom catamarans, founded in 2002 and based in Gdansk, with a second shipyard in Ras Al Khaimah (UAE). It pioneered the luxury flybridge catamaran. Today it builds sail, power and **Eco** (solar-electric) multihulls, from about 18 m up to explorer-size superyachts.

## Lineup (current models)

- **Sunreef 60 / 70 / 80** sailing catamarans (18–24 m / 60–80 ft), with Eco versions.
- **Sunreef Power 60 / 80 / 100**, with Eco versions.
- **Sunreef 100 / 100 Eco** (29.3 m / 96 ft). The **100 Eco is new**: electric propulsion with twin 360 kW motors, a 770 kWh battery and an integrated solar skin.
- **Ultima 88** (28 m / 92 ft), **new**. Performance cat, 10 guests in 5 cabins, with conventional, hybrid or foil-assisted propulsion. A larger **Ultima 111** is in development.
- **Explorer** superyacht projects, such as the Sunreef 65M Explorer.

Ballpark: multi-million USD; prices on request.

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)

- **Sep 2026**: The Sunreef 100 Eco had its world premiere at the Monaco Yacht Show (23–26 Sep).
- **Sep 2026**: At Cannes, the Ultima 88 had its world premiere. It won World Yachts Trophies for Catamaran Best Design, and the 65M Explorer won Best Project. Sunreef reported signing several contracts at the show.
- **Jul 2026**: The Ultima 88 hull was launched from the Ras Al Khaimah yard.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Sunreef sits in the semi-custom luxury tier, above production brands like Lagoon and Fountaine Pajot. Its peers include HH Catamarans and Privilege. Its solar-skin Eco technology is its key difference.
