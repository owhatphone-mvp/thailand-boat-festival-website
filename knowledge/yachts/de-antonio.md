## Identity

De Antonio Yachts is a Spanish day-boat and sport yacht builder founded in 2012 by Marc de Antonio and Stan Chmielewski, headquartered in Barcelona with a commercial presence in Monaco. Privately held by its founders. Their signature design: **hidden (enclosed) outboard engines** — outboards are structurally covered, creating a clean stern, extended swim platform, and maximised deck space. This concept is unique across the entire production range up to 60ft. Present in 47+ dealers across 27+ countries. Per deantonioyachts.com, rnmarine.com, YachtBuyer.

## Lineup (current models, 2024-2026)

- **E23** — 7.2m/24ft. 100% electric catamaran. 50kW motor, 40kWh battery. ~30kt. Official support vessel at 2024 America's Cup. Ballpark EUR 130K.
- **D29** — 8.0m/26ft. Replaces D28. 1-2x 300-400hp Mercury. ~46-50kt. "Wholesale revamp." Ballpark EUR 165K–187K.
- **D32** — 9.9m/33ft. Replaces D34. Twin 225-300hp. 47kt. 2 cabins. Ballpark EUR 300K–400K (est.).
- **D36** — 11.5m/38ft. Replaces D34/D38. Twin up to 400hp (800hp total). 45kt. European Powerboat of the Year 2023. Ballpark EUR 474K.
- **D42** — 11.4m/37ft. Triple 300-400hp. 46kt. 2 cabins. Ballpark USD 799K.
- **D50 Open / Coupe** — 14.9m/49ft (Open), 12.65m/41ft (Coupe). Up to 1,600hp. ~45-50kt. Ballpark EUR 700K–800K.
- **D60 (NEW Oct 2025)** — 18.5m/61ft flagship. 4x 400-600hp Mercury. 46-48kt. 3-4 cabins. Ballpark USD 2.5–3M. One of the largest production yachts relying exclusively on outboards.

Per deantonioyachts.com, yachtworld.com, powerandmotoryacht.com, Yacht Style.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

The D60 flagship launched October 2025 — 18.5m with 4x outboards challenges the assumption that large yachts need inboard diesels. The E23 electric catamaran served as official support vessel at the 2024 America's Cup. De Antonio Yachts Miami subsidiary launched for US entry. D29 and D32 replace the D28 and D34. European Powerboat of the Year twice (D46 in 2019, D36 in 2023) — first Spanish brand to achieve this. Per deantonioyachts.com, marineindustrynews.co.uk.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**Flow Yacht Club** is the exclusive De Antonio distributor for Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, and Thailand. Based in Bangkok and Phuket, they offer the full range from E23 to D60. Asia's first D50 Coupe debuted at Flow Yacht Club, priced from 50M THB (~USD 1.4M). In Hong Kong, **SunAzure** is the exclusive dealer. Per flowyacht.club, Bangkok Post, deantonioyachts.com.

## Positioning vs Competitors

De Antonio's niche is unique: hidden outboards across the entire range up to 60ft. vs. Axopar: both 27-40ft but Axopar is adventure-oriented; De Antonio is Mediterranean lifestyle. vs. Chris-Craft: heritage American inboard vs. modern Spanish outboard. vs. Cap Camarat: De Antonio is significantly more premium. Closest peers: Frauscher (Austria) and Pardo Yachts (Italy).

## DO NOT

- Quote the older D28, D34, D38, D46 as current models — they have been replaced
- State De Antonio uses inboard engines — the entire philosophy is outboard-only (hidden)
- Quote definitive pricing — configuration and engine choices create wide variation
