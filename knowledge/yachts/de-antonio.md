## Identity

Spanish sport-dayboat builder founded in 2012 in Barcelona by Marc de Antonio and Stan Chmielewski; privately held by its founders. Signature: **hidden Mercury outboards** under the aft sunpad across the whole range up to 60ft — clean stern, big swim platform, no exposed engines.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

- **Flow Yacht Club** (Bangkok/Phuket) — exclusive distributor for Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore and Indonesia as of 2026; brought Asia's first D50 Coupe to Thailand and showed it at the Thailand International Boat Show 2025.
- Hong Kong: SunAzure (verify current status).
- The yard has a dedicated APAC/Middle East commercial director and says these regions could reach ~30% of sales in the medium term.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Mediterranean-lifestyle outboard yachts: more design-led than Axopar/Saxdor; closest peers are Pardo and Frauscher. The only production range to hide outboards all the way to 60ft.
