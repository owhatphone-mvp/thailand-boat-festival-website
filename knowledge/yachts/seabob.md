## Identity

SEABOB is the leading premium electric "seascooter" (water sled that runs on and below the surface), made by **CAYAGO AG** in Germany. Not a yacht brand — a luxury water toy found on superyachts, charter yachts and high-end resorts.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

- **CAYAGO Asia Pacific Pte Ltd** (Singapore) manages sales across APAC and publishes USD regional pricing.
- Thailand (as of 2026, verify with SEABOB): **Motor Field** was appointed Thai dealer in Oct 2023; **JY Marine** (Thalang, Phuket) is listed as an official SEABOB partner for sales, service and rentals.
- Other Asian partners include Smooth Marine (Hong Kong), La Marca Marine (Singapore) and Supreme Yachts (Taiwan); the Maldives has hundreds of units at resorts.

## Lineup (current models)

- **SEABOB F9** — current core model (launched Mar 2025, succeeds the F5); 60 TP thrust, ~20kg, three modes (Normal/Sport/Dive), headlights. APAC list ~USD 9,750 (Jul 2025).
- **SEABOB F9 S** — higher-power version, 84 TP, bigger battery (up to ~1 hour sporty use), stability system. APAC list ~USD 13,150 (Jul 2025).
- **SE63 for Automobili Lamborghini** — limited flagship collaboration: 162 TP, up to ~35 km/h with Performance Board, ~35kg, extending side wings. Production from Q2 2026; sold via selected partners from summer 2026. Price on request.
- Older F5 / F5 S / F5 SR units remain common in rental and used fleets.

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)

- **Summer 2026** — SE63 Lamborghini deliveries begin through selected partners.
- **Jan 2026** — SE63 physical debut at boot Düsseldorf, beside a Lamborghini Revuelto.
- **Sep 2025** — SE63 digital launch.
- **Mar 2025** — F9 / F9 S launched (Palm Beach), replacing the F5 generation.

## Positioning vs Competitors

The brand name most people use for the whole category. Cheaper consumer scooters (e.g. Sublue) and technical diving DPVs (Suex, Bonex) serve different buyers; SEABOB focuses on luxury yacht-toy use and design.

## TBF Context

Not a boat exhibitor brand. At past editions, any SEABOB visibility would come via a Thai dealer or as part of a yacht toy package; no confirmed dedicated display on record.

## Talking Points for Sand

- "SEABOB is the German-made electric water sled you see on superyachts — glide on the surface or dive under."
- "The F9 series is current; the F9 S adds power and range. In Asia, list prices start around USD 10K (2025)."
- "The SE63 is a Lamborghini collaboration — the most powerful SEABOB yet."
- "For Thailand, check with SEABOB's Asia office for the current Phuket dealer; Motor Field and JY Marine have both represented the brand."

## DO NOT

- Treat SEABOB as a yacht brand
- Quote prices as final — lists change; Thai taxes/import duties extra
- Call the F5 the current model — the F9 succeeded it
- Confuse with technical diving DPVs
- Name a single definitive Thai dealer without hedging
