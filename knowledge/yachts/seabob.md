## Identity

Seabob is a brand of premium electric underwater scooters (diver propulsion vehicles) manufactured by Cayago AG, a German technology company. Seabob scooters are the world's most recognized luxury underwater sleds, commonly found on superyachts and in the water toy inventories of high-end resorts. While not a yacht brand, Seabob is frequently sold alongside yachts as a premium water toy and is an important part of the superyacht toy ecosystem. Per Seabob official site and Cayago AG.

## Lineup

**F5 Series:**
- **Seabob F5** -- Entry model. Speed up to 11 mph, depth 130ft. Ballpark USD 10,000-12,000. Per Top Jet Surfing.
- **Seabob F5 SR** -- Most powerful F5. Speed 12.4 mph, depth 131ft. Ballpark USD 14,000-16,000. Per Citi Marine Store.

**F7 Series:**
- **Seabob F7** -- Mid-range with enhanced performance over F5.

**F9 Series:**
- **Seabob F9** -- Premium model with advanced battery. Starting ballpark USD 9,500. Per Luxury Marine Toys.
- **Seabob F9S** -- Top of range. 84N thrust, 25.9kg, up to 50 minutes operating time. Per Seabob official site.

**Special Edition:**
- **SE63 for Automobili Lamborghini** -- Designer collaboration model. Per Seabob official site.

All models feature electric propulsion, multiple speed settings, and portable designs suitable for yacht toy decks.

## Recent Moves

Cayago AG has expanded its Asian dealer network through Cayago Asia Pacific (Singapore). New appointments include Motor Field (Thailand), Supreme Yachts (Taiwan), and Global Network (Wuxi, China). Per Yacht Style.

The F9 series is the current flagship. The Lamborghini collaboration (SE63) demonstrates Seabob's positioning as a luxury lifestyle brand. Per Seabob Facebook, 2024.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

Cayago Asia Pacific Pte Ltd (Singapore) manages Seabob distribution across APAC. Per Yachting Pages. New dealer appointments include Motor Field (Thailand), Supreme Yachts (Taiwan), and Global Network (Wuxi, China). Per Yacht Style.

**For Thailand:** Motor Field is the appointed Seabob dealer. Seabob scooters are also commonly sold through yacht dealers as part of complete toy packages. Thailand's yacht charter industry and diving destinations like the Similan Islands are natural environments for Seabob usage.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Seabob operates in the premium underwater scooter segment with limited direct competition. Competitors include Sublue (China, more affordable, consumer-grade), Bonex (Germany, professional-grade DPVs), and Suex (Italy, professional diving). Seabob's unique positioning combines luxury brand appeal, German engineering, designer collaborations (Lamborghini), and strong superyacht industry integration. No competitor matches Seabob's brand recognition in the luxury yacht toy market.
