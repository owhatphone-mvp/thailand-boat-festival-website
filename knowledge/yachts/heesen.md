## Identity
Heesen is a Dutch superyacht builder based in Oss, the Netherlands. It has been building yachts since the late 1970s and is known for fast aluminium yachts and efficient steel displacement yachts of about 50–72m. Since April 2025 it has been privately owned by the family office of Dutch entrepreneur Laurens Last. A Dutch foundation had held the yard after the 2022 sanctions on its former owner.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer
- There is no Thai dealer and no confirmed Asia-Pacific office. As of Sep 2026, Heesen's sales team covers Europe (Oss, Monaco), the Americas (Florida) and the Middle East (a Dubai agent).
- Asian buyers usually work through the yard directly or through international superyacht brokers.
- Heesen has highlighted Asia as a growth market. Its under-500GT yachts suit many Asian cruising grounds.

## Lineup (ballpark only)
Heesen offers three build routes: **Series** (built on speculation, fast delivery), **Smart Custom** and **Full Custom**.
- **50m (164ft) aluminium, 499GT**: fast, ~23 knots. Example: *Sophia*, for 2026 delivery. Ballpark USD 30–45M.
- **50–56m (164–184ft) steel**: displacement yachts at ~15.5 knots. Ballpark USD 35–55M.
- **55–57m (180–187ft) aluminium**: about 780GT, ~18–21 knots.
- **72m (236ft) steel**: *Ananda*, 1,750GT, due in 2030. This is a flagship-scale project.
- Pre-owned Heesens are traded widely through brokers.

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)
- **Sep 2026:** Commercial team expanded ahead of the autumn shows. A 2023 Heesen (*Reliance*, 55m) was at Monaco Yacht Show 2026.
- **Jul 2026:** Keel laid for the 56m steel Project Kansei (anniversary edition). The 57m all-aluminium YN 20857 was launched for repeat clients.
- **Jun 2026:** First delivery of 2026 — the 55m Smart Custom *Pa'lante*.
- **Jun 2025:** Heesen dropped on-land stands at Monaco and Fort Lauderdale and now shows its yachts in the water.
- **Apr 2025:** Acquired by Laurens Last's family office. Revenue for 2024 was a record ~€209M.
- **Mar 2025:** 55m steel *Serena* delivered.

## Positioning vs Competitors
Heesen is a leader among 50–60m Dutch yachts, competing with Amels and Benetti. Its edge is aluminium speed and efficient hull forms, delivered more quickly than full-custom yards.
