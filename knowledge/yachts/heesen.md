## Identity

Heesen Yachts is a Dutch superyacht builder founded in 1957, headquartered in Oss, Netherlands. The yard specialises in semi-custom and fully custom superyachts ranging from 30 metres to over 80 metres, with a particular reputation for aluminium construction and fast displacement hull forms (FDHF). Heesen was acquired by Dutch entrepreneur and investor Laurens Last, who completed full ownership in 2025. Per Superyacht News and Yacht Harbour, 2025.

## Lineup

Heesen offers both semi-custom series and fully custom builds:

**Aluminium Series (FDHF):**
- **50m Aluminium** -- Fast-cruising series, the first full-custom aluminium superyacht platform. FDHF hull delivers high speeds with fuel efficiency. Ballpark pricing from USD 25-35M. Per Heesen official site and YachtBuyer.

**Steel Series:**
- **55m Steel (FDHF)** -- Fast displacement motor yacht. First hull sold in 2024. Serena (YN 20555) was the first delivery in 2025. Ballpark pricing from USD 35-45M. Per Boat International.
- **60m+ Steel** -- Larger custom steel projects, fully custom specification.

**Galactica Range:**
- **Galactica Super Nova (70m)** -- The largest Heesen ever built, delivered as Solemates. A showcase of the yard's capabilities at the top end. Per SuperYachts.com and Boat International.

Pre-owned Heesen yachts are listed from approximately USD 12.5M for smaller vessels. Per Romeo United Yachts.

## Recent Moves

**New ownership (2025):** Laurens Last completed full acquisition of Heesen Yachts, bringing new capital and strategic direction. Per Superyacht News, 2025.

**Serena (55m Steel):** First delivery of 2025, a 55m steel FDHF yacht. Part of the new 55m Steel Series. Per Powerboat World, 2025.

**Orion (50m Aluminium):** Delivered 2025, part of the fast-cruising Aluminium Series. Per YachtBuyer.

**Galactica Super Nova (70m):** Heesen's largest build, delivered as Solemates. Per Boat International.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

Heesen has no traditional dealer network but works with international brokers and has actively targeted the Asian market. The yard published a dedicated article on "Exploring the untapped potential of Asia's yachting market," highlighting the region's 1,533 billionaires as potential clients. Heesen notes that its "high quality 499GT yachts offer an excellent choice" for Asia, as sub-500GT yachts have regulatory advantages in many Asian jurisdictions. Per Heesen Yachts official article. Thailand's new charter licensing regime is a positive development for the superyacht market in the region.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Heesen competes in the semi-custom and custom superyacht segment. Direct competitors include Amels/Damen (Netherlands, semi-custom Limited Editions), Benetti (Italy, broader range to 100m+), and Feadship (Netherlands, full-custom only, higher price point). Heesen's differentiator is aluminium expertise combined with FDHF hull technology, offering speed and efficiency that steel competitors cannot match. The semi-custom approach also means shorter build times and more predictable pricing than fully custom yards.

## TBF Context
