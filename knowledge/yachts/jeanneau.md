## Identity

Jeanneau (Chantiers Jeanneau) is a French production yacht builder founded 1957, headquartered in Les Herbiers, Vendée. Part of **Groupe Beneteau** since 1991, the world's largest boatbuilder by volume (EUR 1.03B FY2024). Covers sail and power in entry-to-mid tier; often described as offering the best sailing performance among the big-three production builders (Jeanneau, Beneteau, Bavaria). Per jeanneau.com, Groupe Beneteau investor relations.

## Lineup (current models, 2024-2026)

**Sail:**
- **Sun Odyssey** — cruising monohulls, the volume range. 350 to 490 (10.4–14.4m / 34–47ft). New: Sun Odyssey 415 and 455 (Marc Lombard designs). Ballpark USD 150K–550K.
- **Jeanneau Yachts (premium)** — elevated sub-brand with superyacht-designer involvement. JY55 (17m) and JY60 (18.3m). Designed by Philippe Briand, interiors by Andrew Winch. JY60 offers 19 layout combinations. Ballpark USD 1.05M–1.5M.
- **Sun Fast** — performance / offshore racing. 30 OD, 3300, 3600. Niche range, not typically relevant to luxury buyers.

**Power:**
- **Cap Camarat** — walkarounds (5.5–12.5m). USD 50K–600K+.
- **Merry Fisher** — cabin cruisers (5.7–12.6m). New 1295 Coupe (2025). USD 40K–550K+.
- **Leader** — sport cruisers (8–12.3m). V-hull by Michael Peters. USD 150K–600K+.
- **NC** — weekenders (8–13m). USD 150K–750K.
- **Velasco 43** — trawler-sport hybrid. USD 395K–600K.
- **DB/37** — day boat (11.8m). Garroni Design. USD 400K–550K.

Per jeanneau.com, Boat24, YachtBuyer, broker listings, 2024-2026.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

Groupe Beneteau entered a destocking cycle (revenue EUR 1.03B FY2024 → EUR 849M FY2025). Strategy: "premiumization" with 66 new models by 2028. Biggest move: **E-Lektra Marine** (April 2026), a JV with Fountaine Pajot Group for electric/hybrid propulsion (9-24m boats) — targeting 10-15% of the global sailing market by 2030. Covers Jeanneau, Beneteau, Dufour, Lagoon, Excess, Fountaine Pajot. Per Groupe Beneteau, plugboats.com.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**Boat Lagoon Yachting (BLY)** is Jeanneau's sole authorized distributor for Thailand and Singapore, handling sales, charter, and after-sales from Boat Lagoon Marina, Phuket. BLY is also Princess's SE Asia distributor — one of the most important multi-brand dealers in the region. In Hong Kong, Groupe Beneteau ended its long Simpson Marine relationship in March 2024, appointing Asiamarine and NextWave Yachting as new dealers — Jeanneau's specific HK dealer post-split unconfirmed. Per boatlagoonyachting.com, Yacht Style, 2024-2026.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Jeanneau sits in the "production yacht" category alongside Beneteau and Bavaria. Within Groupe Beneteau, Jeanneau leans sailing performance; Beneteau is more design-forward. vs. Bavaria: better upwind performance; Bavaria = more volume, lower prices. vs. Hanse: more accessible pricing; Hanse = premium production. The Jeanneau Yachts sub-brand competes with Oyster and Hallberg-Rassy at lower prices but less exclusivity.

## DO NOT

- Confuse Jeanneau with Jeanneau Yachts (the premium sub-brand) — they are different tiers within the same marque
- Quote definitive pricing — Jeanneau rarely publishes retail prices; figures are broker estimates
- State Jeanneau was "founded 140 years ago" — that refers to Groupe Beneteau's combined heritage (Beneteau founded 1884); Jeanneau was founded 1957
- Oversell exclusivity — Jeanneau is a production builder; clients seeking uniqueness should look at semi-custom yards
