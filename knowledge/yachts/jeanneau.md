## Identity

French production builder founded 1957 in Les Herbiers, Vendée; part of **Groupe Beneteau** (one of the world's largest boatbuilders, about EUR 849M revenue in 2025) since 1995. Builds sail and power, from dayboats to the premium Jeanneau Yachts line.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

As of 2026, **Boat Lagoon Yachting (BLY)** at Boat Lagoon Marina, Phuket, represents Jeanneau in Thailand (sales, charter, service). BLY also handles Princess and other brands. For other Asian markets, check jeanneau.com for the current dealer.

## Lineup (current models)

- **Sun Odyssey**: cruising monohulls, roughly 10.4–14.3 m (34–47 ft). Includes the 415 and 455 and the **new 385** (Cannes 2026). Ballpark USD 150K–550K.
- **Jeanneau Yachts**: premium sailing line (55/60/65, 17–20 m / 55–65 ft), designed by Philippe Briand. The new **JY55 Expedition** premiered at Cannes 2026. Ballpark USD 1M–2M+.
- **Cap Camarat** walkarounds and center consoles (6–12.5 m). The new **6.0 CC** came out in 2026. Ballpark USD 50K–600K.
- **Merry Fisher** cabin cruisers (6–12.6 m). Ballpark USD 40K–550K.
- **NC / Leader** weekenders and sport cruisers (8–13 m). Ballpark USD 150K–750K.
- **DB/37** dayboat (12 m). Ballpark USD 400K–550K.
- **New 2026 power**: **EX 34** dayboat (10.4 m / 34 ft, twin outboards), **TH33 / TH38** power catamarans (10.2 / 11.8 m), and the **Sea Loft 480** (14.1 m / 46 ft), a "floating villa" built for life at anchor.

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)

- **Sep 2026**: Cannes premieres: EX 34, Sea Loft 480 (concept to production), Sun Odyssey 385, JY55 Expedition. The group launched 24 new models in total.
- **H1 2026**: Group retail sales up about 14% year on year; 66 new models planned for 2025–2027.
- **Apr 2026**: Groupe Beneteau and Fountaine Pajot Group formed **E-Lektra Marine**, a 50/50 JV for electric/hybrid sail propulsion (9–24 m boats) covering Jeanneau and six other brands.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Volume production builder alongside Beneteau and Bavaria, leaning toward sailing performance; Jeanneau Yachts competes with premium semi-custom yards at a lower price.

## DO NOT

- Confuse Jeanneau with Jeanneau Yachts, the premium sailing line.
- Quote definitive prices. Figures are ballpark broker estimates.
- Say Jeanneau is "140 years old". That is Beneteau's 1884 heritage; Jeanneau was founded in 1957.
- Oversell exclusivity; it is a production builder.
- Say or imply that Jeanneau or its dealer will exhibit at TBF 2027.
