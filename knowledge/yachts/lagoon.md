## Identity

Lagoon is the world's leading production catamaran builder, part of Groupe Beneteau, with over 7,000 catamarans built since its founding in France in 1984. The brand dominates the 38-to-78-foot segment and is the most specified brand in global charter fleets, combining accessible pricing with proven hull designs refined over decades.

## Lineup (current models, 2024-2026)

**Lagoon 43** (2024): Newest model, redesigned hull and living spaces, single-level living concept. Nominated for SAIL Top 10 Best Boats 2025. Ballpark: mid-six-figures USD.

**Lagoon 46**: Proven mid-range cruiser widely used in charter fleets. Four cabins, strong resale values.

**Lagoon 51**: Sweet spot for owner-operator and premium charter use. Updated with sustainable luxury interior options, per Lagoon's dealer network, 2025.

**Lagoon 55**: Bluewater cruiser with enhanced performance hulls for long-distance passages.

**Lagoon 60**: Gateway to the premium range with semi-custom interiors and crew quarters. First APAC unit sold to a Thai client through ASIAMARINE, 2024.

**Seventy 8** (78 ft): Flagship in sail and power configurations. Multi-million USD pricing. Per YachtWorld.

**MY Lagoon 4**: Power catamaran variant for owners preferring power over sail.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

The Lagoon 43 launch marked a design evolution with a new hull platform, not merely a refresh. The E-Lektra Marine joint venture (announced April 2026 between Groupe Beneteau and Fountaine Pajot Group) covers seven sailing brands including Lagoon, targeting electric and hybrid propulsion across the fleet, per Groupe Beneteau press release. Lagoon has offered hybrid-electric options on select models, though full range electrification remains a longer-term goal.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

ASIAMARINE has served as the exclusive Lagoon dealer for Hong Kong, Thailand, and Singapore since early 2024. Based at Phuket Boat Lagoon Marina, ASIAMARINE won Best Asia Pacific Lagoon Dealer after selling the first Lagoon 60 in the region. They also organized the Lagoon Escapade Thailand event in 2024, per Yacht Style. Lagoon is well represented in Thai charter operations, particularly in Phuket and the Andaman Sea.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Lagoon is the volume leader in production catamarans, competing directly with Fountaine Pajot (premium production), Leopard Catamarans (Robertson & Caine, South Africa), and Bali (Catana Group, France). Lagoon's advantages are scale, dealer reach, charter fleet penetration, and resale liquidity. Fountaine Pajot positions above Lagoon on finish quality and interior specification; Sunreef occupies a higher custom-build tier. Within Groupe Beneteau, the Excess brand targets a performance-oriented catamaran buyer.

## Talking Points for Sand

- Lagoon is the world's number one production catamaran brand with unmatched global service infrastructure.
- The Lagoon 43 is a brand-new model with redesigned hull and living spaces, ideal for buyers entering the catamaran lifestyle.
- ASIAMARINE in Phuket provides dedicated Lagoon sales, service, and sea trial experiences for Thai-based buyers.
- Lagoon catamarans hold strong resale value and are the most common brand in charter fleets worldwide, making them a practical choice for owner-charter programs.
- The range spans from the accessible 43 to the flagship Seventy 8, allowing buyers to step up within the brand.
- Hybrid and electric propulsion options are expanding across the range via the E-Lektra Marine initiative.

## DO NOT

- Confuse Lagoon with Excess (the performance catamaran brand also under Groupe Beneteau).
- Imply Lagoon is a custom or semi-custom builder; it is a production brand with limited customization options on larger models.
- Quote definitive pricing; always provide ballpark USD ranges and note variability by configuration.
- Name specific owners of Lagoon catamarans in the Thai market.
