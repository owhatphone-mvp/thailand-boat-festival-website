## Identity

Lagoon is the world's best-selling production catamaran brand. It was founded in France in 1984, belongs to Groupe Beneteau, and has built more than 7,000 catamarans. It is the most common brand in global charter fleets and covers sail catamarans from 11 to 25 m plus power catamarans.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**ASIAMARINE** has been Lagoon's exclusive dealer for Thailand, Hong Kong and Singapore since 2024. It is based at Boat Lagoon Marina, Phuket (as of 2026). It sold the first Lagoon 60 in the Asia-Pacific region to a client in Thailand and runs Lagoon owner events such as the Lagoon Escapade Thailand.

## Lineup (current models)

- **Lagoon 38** (11.4 m / 37 ft): the new entry model, unveiled in 2026. Ballpark USD 400K–550K.
- **Lagoon 43** (2024): single-level living. Ballpark USD 600K–800K.
- **Lagoon 47** (15.0 m / 49 ft): **new, world premiere at Cannes in Sep 2026**. It replaces the 46, comes with 3–5 cabins, and was designed by VPLP (exterior) and Nauta (interior). Ballpark USD 900K–1.2M.
- **Lagoon 51, 55 and 60**: owner and premium-charter models, with crew options on the larger boats. Ballpark USD 1.1M–2.5M.
- **SIXTY 5** and **EIGHTY 2** (25 m / 82 ft): the luxury flagship sail catamarans. Price multi-million USD, on request.
- **EIGHTY 3** (24.4 m / 80 ft): the **new flagship power catamaran (2026)**. It has 4–6 cabins, a range of up to about 3,500 nm, and was designed by VPLP, Patrick Le Quément and Nauta.

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)

- **Sep 2026**: Full range at Cannes; Lagoon 47 world premiere, EIGHTY 3 power cat shown.
- **Apr 2026**: **E-Lektra Marine** JV (Groupe Beneteau + Fountaine Pajot) for electric/hybrid propulsion, Lagoon included.
- **Mar 2026**: The Lagoon 38 and EIGHTY 3 were unveiled at the Lagoon Exclusive Days in Bordeaux.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Lagoon is the volume leader in catamarans. Its strengths are scale, dealer reach, charter-fleet presence and resale liquidity. Fountaine Pajot, Leopard and Bali are its production peers, while Sunreef sits in a higher semi-custom tier.

## DO NOT

- Confuse Lagoon with Excess, the group's performance catamaran brand.
- Describe Lagoon as a custom builder. It is a production builder, with semi-custom options only on its largest models.
- Quote definitive prices; give ballpark ranges only.
- Name owners.
- Say or imply that Lagoon or ASIAMARINE will exhibit at TBF 2027.
