## Identity

Saxdor Yachts is a Finnish dayboat brand founded in 2019 by **Sakari Mattila** — the serial entrepreneur who also founded Aquador (1994), XO Boats (2008), and Axopar (2012) before leaving Axopar in 2018. HQ in Helsinki; manufactured in Elk, Poland. Acquired by **Malibu Boats** (USA, NASDAQ: MBUU) on 10 March 2026 for EUR 150M (~USD 175M). Saxdor retains brand autonomy; Mattila stays as Head Designer, CEO Erna Rusi continues. Revenue reached EUR 180M in 2025 (+65% YoY), making it arguably Europe's fastest-growing boat brand. **Not a sister brand to Axopar** — they share a founder but have no corporate relationship. Per saxdoryachts.com, Malibu Boats SEC filings, Powerboat & Rib.

- **200 / 205** — 5.9m/20ft entry level. Single 115-150hp. From EUR 27K. Award-winning bow rider.
- **270 GTO** — 7.9m/26ft. Single 250hp or twin options. From EUR 84K. Sportboat.
- **320 GTC / GTO** — 10.3m/34ft. Twin 200-600hp. GTC = enclosed; GTO = open sport. From EUR 149K–163K.
- **340 GTWA** — ~10m/34ft walkaround. Twin 250hp. From EUR 175K.
- **400 GTS / GTO / GTC** — 12.4m/41ft. Twin 300-350hp. Fold-down terraces, electric sunroof. From EUR 245K–299K.
- **460 GTC (NEW 2026)** — 14.4m/47ft flagship. Triple 300-425hp. Sleeps 8, 3 cabins, fold-down balconies, 48V system. Boot Düsseldorf 2026. Yacht Style Award. From EUR 499K.

Per saxdoryachts.com, YachtBuyer, DCH Marine, Boot Düsseldorf coverage.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

The Malibu Boats acquisition (March 2026, EUR 150M) is the headline — Malibu's first move into cruisers/dayboats and Europe. Revenue hit EUR 180M in 2025 with 65% growth. The 460 GTC flagship debuted January 2026. Sakari Mattila won the Judges' Special Award at the Motor Boat Awards 2026 for his career spanning five boat brands. Per saxdoryachts.com, Clairfield, Powerboat & Rib, Yacht Style.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**DCH Marine** was appointed exclusive APAC distributor in February 2024, covering Greater China, SE Asia, and Australia/NZ. Based at Royal Phuket Marina. However, **Motor Field Thailand** — original Saxdor dealer since November 2021 — remains active and represented Saxdor at TBF 2026 (400 GTO, 340 GTWA, 320 GTC). The DCH/Motor Field relationship is not publicly clarified. Per DCH Marine, saxdoryachts.com, Yacht Style.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Saxdor's signature differentiators from Axopar: fold-down side terraces (extending deck space at anchor), electric opening sunroofs, and more aggressive pricing at comparable sizes. Where Axopar prioritises rough-sea performance and modular adventure configurations, Saxdor emphasises at-anchor comfort and lifestyle entertaining. vs. Nimbus: Saxdor is more modern and performance-oriented; Nimbus is traditional Swedish cruising. vs. XO Boats: ironic since Mattila also founded XO — XO uses aluminium for ruggedness; Saxdor uses GRP for performance and finish.
