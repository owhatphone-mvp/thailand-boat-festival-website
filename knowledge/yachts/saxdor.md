## Identity

Finnish dayboat brand founded in 2019 by Sakari Mattila (who earlier founded Aquador, XO Boats and Axopar). HQ Helsinki; built in Elk, Poland and Larsmo, Finland. Part of **Malibu Boats** (USA, NASDAQ: MBUU) since March 2026 (EUR 150M deal); brand runs autonomously under CEO Erna Rusi. **Not a sister brand to Axopar** — shared founder only.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

- **DCH Marine** — exclusive Asia-Pacific distributor since Feb 2024 (Greater China, SE Asia, Australia/NZ); ran Saxdor owner/demo events in Hong Kong (June 2026) and Singapore (July 2026).
- **Motor Field** (Phuket) was Saxdor's original Thai dealer (from 2021); showed a 320 GTO at Thailand Int'l Boat Show 2025. Its role alongside DCH is unclarified as of 2026 — tell clients to confirm the Thai sales point with the brand.

## Lineup (current models)

- **200 / 205** — 5.9m/20ft bowriders. Ballpark USD 30K–50K.
- **270 GTO** — 7.9m/26ft sport dayboat. Ballpark USD 90K–130K.
- **320 GTO / GTC** — 10.3m/34ft open or enclosed. Ballpark USD 160K–250K.
- **340 GTWA** — ~10m/34ft walkaround. Ballpark USD 190K–260K.
- **400 GTO / GTC / GTS** — 12.4m/41ft, fold-down terraces, electric sunroof. Ballpark USD 270K–400K.
- **460 GTC** — ~14m/47ft flagship, enclosed wheelhouse, 3 cabins, triple outboards. Ballpark USD 550K+.
- **460 GTS (NEW)** — ~14m/47ft open-wheelhouse flagship sister; optional sliding doors. Price on request.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Closest rival is Axopar: Axopar leans to rough-water adventure and modularity, Saxdor to at-anchor lifestyle (fold-down terraces, sunroofs) at sharp prices.
