## Identity

Sunseeker International is a British motor yacht builder headquartered in Poole, Dorset, founded in 1979 by Robert Braithwaite. Known for aggressive, performance-oriented styling and sport yachts in the 17–50m (55–161ft) range. After 11 years under China's Wanda Group (2013–2024), the brand was sold in late 2024 to a KCP-led consortium (Lionheart Capital and Orienta Capital Partners). That buyout subsequently collapsed in early 2026, leaving secured lenders (Cheyne Capital / Cross Ocean Partners) in control as of mid-2026. Steve Timms, a 25-year Sunseeker veteran and former COO, serves as interim CEO. Per sunseeker.com and Boat International, May 2026.

## Lineup (current models, 2024-2026)

- **Predator** — sport cruiser, open profile. Predator 55 (17m/55ft, debuted Miami 2026). Ballpark USD 2–2.5M.
- **Manhattan** — flybridge cruisers, the volume range. Manhattan 55/56 (17m/55ft), Manhattan 68 (21m/68ft), Manhattan 80 (25m/80ft). Manhattan 56 world premiere at boot Düsseldorf 2026. Ballpark USD 2.5–8M.
- **90 Ocean** — standalone ocean-cruising model, ~27m/90ft. Broader beam than Manhattan range, designed for longer passages. Ballpark USD 8–12M.
- **Sport Yacht** — performance hardtop. Models in 65–76ft range. Ballpark USD 3–6M.
- **Yacht & Superyacht** — semi-custom above 100ft. 116 Yacht, 134 Superyacht. Ballpark USD 15–25M+.

Predator 55 and Manhattan 56 are the newest additions (2025-2026). Per sunseeker.com, YachtBuyer, Yacht Style.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

Three ownership changes in under two years define the current story. Wanda Group exited November 2024, selling to a KCP-led consortium. That transaction collapsed in early 2026, with secured lenders taking control. Interim CEO Steve Timms leads. Product-wise, the Manhattan 56 and Predator 55 show continued investment. Per Boat International, Marine Industry News, Yacht Style.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**DCH Marine** is the exclusive Sunseeker distributor for all of Asia-Pacific, appointed by Sunseeker International. Led by Adam Waters, DCH Marine is headquartered in Hong Kong with offices in Shenzhen, Singapore, Phuket, and Australia. They also distribute Saxdor and Williams Jet Tenders across the region. In Thailand, DCH Marine operates from **Royal Phuket Marina**. Per sunseeker.com dealer locator, Yacht Style, DCH Marine website, 2025-2026.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Sunseeker is the "thrill" end of British luxury yachts — sport performance and aggressive styling. Princess emphasises comfort and refinement; Sunseeker leans into speed and extroverted character. Azimut offers a broader Italian range at similar prices; Sunseeker competes most directly with the S Collection and Flybridge lines. Pershing matches on performance but at higher prices.

## TBF Context

Sunseeker exhibited at **TBF 2026** through DCH Marine. Models shown: **90 Ocean** and **Manhattan 55**. The 90 Ocean was a standout display. Per sunseeker.com, Yacht Style, 2026.
