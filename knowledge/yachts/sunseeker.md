## Identity
British motor yacht builder from Poole, Dorset, founded in 1969. Known for sporty, performance-led styling from ~17m to ~30m. Since July 2026 its long-term owners are investment firms Cross Ocean Partners and Cheyne Capital, with Scott Millar named incoming CEO.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer
- **DCH Marine** (Hong Kong-based) is Sunseeker's Asia-Pacific dealer as of 2026.
- In **March 2026** DCH Marine opened an office at **Royal Phuket Marina** to serve Thailand and the wider region (sales, after-sales, client care).
- For other Asian markets, verify the current dealer on sunseeker.com — the network has changed several times.

## Lineup (current models, selected)
- **Performance** — Superhawk 55, Predator 55 (17.3m / 56ft). Ballpark USD 2–3M.
- **Sport Yacht** — 65 and 75 Sport Yacht (~20–23m / 65–75ft). Ballpark USD 3–6M.
- **Manhattan** (flybridge) — Manhattan 56, 68, 94 (~17–28.6m / 56–94ft). Ballpark USD 2.5–15M.
- **Ocean** (long-range) — 82 Ocean, 90 Ocean (~25–27m / 82–90ft). Ballpark USD 7–12M.
- **Yacht** — 76 Yacht, 100 Yacht (~23–30m / 76–100ft). Ballpark USD 5–18M.

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)
- **Sep 2026 (Cannes):** world premiere of the **Manhattan 94** (28.6m, up to 26 knots, 10 guests, full-beam owner's suite) — new Manhattan flagship; hull two already sold. Nine-model display.
- **Jul 2026:** Cross Ocean Partners and Cheyne Capital confirmed as long-term owners; Scott Millar (turnaround specialist) appointed CEO; Adrian Mardell named non-executive chairman.
- **Apr 2026:** planned KCP-led takeover fell through; Steve Timms (25-year Sunseeker veteran) became interim CEO.
- **Mar 2026:** DCH Marine opened its Royal Phuket Marina office.
- **Jan 2026:** Manhattan 56 world premiere at boot Düsseldorf.
