## Identity
Pershing is the high-performance sport yacht brand of **Ferretti Group**, built in Mondolfo, Italy. Founded in 1985 (40th anniversary in 2025) and part of Ferretti Group since 2004, it is known for aggressive styling, lightweight construction and very high speeds. Since 2022 its GTX "Sport Utility Yacht" line adds more outdoor space and comfort.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer
- **V Yachts Asia** has been the Thailand dealer for Pershing (with Ferretti Yachts and Riva) since 2021, based at Phuket Boat Lagoon with a Pattaya office; still active in 2026 — verify current coverage with Ferretti Group.
- Elsewhere in Asia, Pershing is sold via Ferretti Group's Asia-Pacific network and local dealers.

## Lineup (current models)
- **X series** (pure performance) — 5X, 6X, 7X, 8X, 9X (~16.5–28m / 54–92ft); top speeds roughly 40–50 knots depending on model. Ballpark USD 2–14M.
- **GTX series** (Sport Utility Yachts) — GTX70 (21.8m / 71ft), GTX80 (24m / 79ft), **GTX90** (28m / 92ft), GTX116 (35.2m / 115ft). Ballpark USD 4–20M.
- **Pershing 140** — 43m / 141ft aluminium flagship. Ballpark USD 20M+.

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)
- **Sep 2026 (Cannes):** world premiere of the **GTX90** — 28m, twin 2,200hp MAN V12s with waterjets, ~35 knots, 33 sq m sportsbridge, garage for tender and jet ski, four guest cabins. Fourth GTX model; first hull launched July 2026.
- **May 2026:** parent Ferretti Group named Stassi Anastassov CEO.
- **2025:** 40th anniversary; **GTX70** world debut at the Venice Boat Show — the smallest GTX.

## Positioning vs Competitors
The performance flagship of Italian yachting. Sister brand Wally is equally avant-garde; Sunseeker's Predator line is a British sporty alternative; the GTX line competes with more comfort-oriented sport yachts while keeping Pershing speed.

## DO NOT
- Call Pershing a generic "motor yacht" — it is a performance/sport yacht brand
- Confuse the X series, the GTX series and the 140 — different platforms
- Say every Pershing uses waterjets — propulsion varies by model
- Present Pershing as a long-range displacement cruiser
- Quote definitive prices or name owners
- Say Pershing or V Yachts Asia will exhibit at TBF 2027
