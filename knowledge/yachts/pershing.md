## Identity

Pershing is the performance sport yacht brand within **Ferretti Group**, often called the "Ferrari of the seas" for its aggressive styling, carbon fibre construction, and waterjet propulsion above 40 knots. Founded in 1985 by Tilli Antonelli in Mondolfo, Italy, acquired by Ferretti Group in 2004. Celebrates its 40th anniversary in 2025. Every model prioritises speed, lightweight engineering, and visceral driving experience. Per pershing-yacht.com, Boat International, Yacht Style.

## Lineup (current models, 2024-2026)

- **5X** — 16.5m/54ft. Carbon fibre. Twin Volvo IPS. 36kn. Entry sport. Ballpark USD 2-2.5M.
- **6X** — 18.9m/62ft. Carbon/GRP. Twin MAN V12 1550hp. 48kn. Ballpark USD 3-4.5M.
- **GTX70** (NEW 2025) — 21.8m/72ft. Triple Volvo IPS 900hp. 40th anniversary model. Ballpark USD 4-5.5M.
- **8X** — 25.6m/84ft. Carbon fibre. 50kn+. Ballpark USD 7-10M.
- **9X** — 28.1m/92ft. Twin MTU 2638hp. 42kn. Ballpark USD 10-14M.
- **140** — 43.3m/142ft. Aluminium. Four MTU 2600hp. 38kn. Flagship. From ~USD 21M+.

Per pershing-yacht.com, YachtBuyer, Boat International, YachtWorld, 2024-2026.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

The **GTX70** launched in 2025 as the 40th anniversary model — a new product line between the 6X and 8X, designed for clients wanting Pershing performance with more liveability. The **Pershing 140** continues accumulating hulls — the fourth featured a bespoke "VIP area" and the fifth was in build as of April 2025. At 43m and 38 knots, the 140 is among the fastest production superyachts. The 6X remains a standout with 48 knots, among the fastest in its class. Per pershing-yacht.com, Boat International, Yacht Style, 2025.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**V Yachts Asia** is the exclusive Ferretti Group dealer for Thailand, covering Pershing alongside Ferretti Yachts and Riva, based at **Phuket Boat Lagoon**. Pershing's high-performance positioning resonates in the Thai market, where day trips between islands and high-speed cruising align with the brand's DNA. **Simpson Marine** handles the broader APAC from Hong Kong. The Pershing 5X and 6X are the most practical models for Southeast Asian waters due to their manageable size. Per V Yachts Asia, Simpson Marine, Yacht Style, 2024-2026.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Pershing has no true equal. Carbon fibre, waterjet propulsion, and 40-50 knot speeds in a luxury finish is unique. **Mangusta** (Overmarine) comes closest but leans towards volume. **Wally** (also Ferretti Group) is equally fast but more avant-garde. **Sunseeker Predator** offers British sport performance at lower speeds. The 140 competes alone — a 43m planing sport yacht with superyacht accommodation.

Pershing was represented at **TBF 2026** through V Yachts Asia as part of the Ferretti Group trio (Ferretti, Riva, Pershing). The Pershing 5X or 6X would be the most likely models shown given regional practicality. Per V Yachts Asia, Yacht Style, 2026.

## DO NOT

- Call Pershing a "motor yacht" generically — it is a sport yacht / performance yacht
- Confuse the X series (5X, 6X, 8X, 9X) with the 140 (different platform, aluminium vs carbon)
- Quote definitive pricing — bespoke interiors create wide variation
- Suggest Pershing is suitable for long-range displacement cruising — it is a planing hull brand
