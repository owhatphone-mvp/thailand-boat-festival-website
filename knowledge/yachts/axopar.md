## Identity

Axopar Boats is a Finnish builder founded in 2014 and based in the Helsinki area. It created the "adventure boat" category: twin-step, deep-V day cruisers from 22 to 45 ft. It has delivered more than 7,600 boats and had about EUR 170M in turnover in 2025. Hulls are built in Poland at Markos, a yard Axopar has owned a majority of since Jun 2024.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

As of 2026, **Derani Yachts** is Axopar's exclusive dealer for Thailand, Malaysia and Singapore. Its Thai base is Boat Lagoon Marina in Phuket, its Malaysian office at Pangkor Marina opened in Oct 2025, and Singapore is covered through partner representation. In Hong Kong and China, the dealer is **Simpson Marine** (since Aug 2024).

## Lineup (current models)

- **22 Spyder / T-Top** (7.2 m / 24 ft): single outboard. Ballpark USD 80K–130K.
- **25 Cross Top** (8 m / 26 ft). Ballpark USD 125K–160K.
- **29 XC / CCX / Sun Top** (9.3 m / 31 ft). Ballpark USD 260K–350K.
- **38 range** (11.85 m / 39 ft): the **new generation succeeding the 37**, which sold more than 3,000 units. Versions: XC Cross Cabin, Sun Top, **Cross Top (new)**; twin 300–350 hp, 45+ kn. Ballpark USD 450K–600K.
- **45 XC / Cross Top** (13.9 m / 46 ft): the flagship, with twin or triple outboards. Ballpark USD 725K–1M.
- **AX/E** electric models: 22 and 25 sizes. Check current availability with the dealer.
- **BRABUS Shadow** luxury-performance versions built on Axopar hulls. The new **Shadow 1000** (38 ft, 2 × 500 hp, about 60 kn) premiered at Cannes in 2026.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Axopar leads the Nordic adventure dayboat segment, with a rough-water hull and modular decks. Saxdor, also Finnish, focuses more on comfort at anchor. De Antonio offers a Mediterranean-lifestyle take on the same size range.

## TBF Context

At the last edition (TBF 2026), Derani Yachts showed five Axopars: the 37 XC, a 29 CCX (its Thailand premiere), the 29 XC, the 22 T-Top and the 22 Spyder.
