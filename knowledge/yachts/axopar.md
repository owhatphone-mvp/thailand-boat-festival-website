## Identity

Axopar Boats Oy is a Finnish day-cruiser brand founded in 2014 by Jan-Erik Viitala and Albert Zaleski, headquartered in Helsinki. Privately held by its founders — **not owned by Nimbus Group** (Nimbus is a Swedish reseller only, since 2015). Known for pioneering the "adventure boat" category with twin-step deep-V hulls in the 22–45ft range. Over 7,500 boats delivered. Hulls manufactured in Poland by Markos Sp. z o.o. (majority acquired by Axopar June 2024 for vertical integration), with final assembly in Finland. Per axopar.com, ibinews.com, Yacht Style.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

Axopar acquired majority stake in Markos (Polish hull OEM) in June 2024, vertically integrating production. The 38 XC debuted at boot Düsseldorf January 2026. The AX/E electric line launched with Evoy powertrains. **BRABUS Marine** partnership (since 2017) continues: BRABUS Shadow models (300/500/900/1200/1500) are high-performance luxury conversions of Axopar hulls, with Shadow 1500 (based on Axopar 45) premiering at Monaco Yacht Show 2025 at ~USD 1.75M. "BRABUS Line" trim packages available on standard models. Per axopar.com, brabus.com, Yacht Style, Superyachts.com.

Per axopar.com dealer page, derani-yachts.com, simpsonmarine.com, Yacht Style.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Axopar is the pioneer of the Nordic adventure dayboat — twin-step hull for rough-sea confidence, modular cockpit configurations (open, cabin, sun top, cross cabin), Scandinavian functional design. vs. Saxdor (also Finnish, same original designer Sakari Mattila but **no corporate relationship**): Axopar focuses on performance in choppy conditions; Saxdor emphasises at-anchor comfort with fold-down terraces. vs. Chris-Craft: completely different ethos — Chris-Craft is heritage American luxury; Axopar is modern Scandinavian utility. vs. De Antonio: both compete in 27-40ft but De Antonio is Mediterranean lifestyle, Axopar is adventure-oriented.
