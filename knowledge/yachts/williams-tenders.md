## Identity

Williams Performance Tenders is a British manufacturer of luxury jet-drive tenders and chase boats, founded in 1996 and based in Oxfordshire, UK. The company is the world's leading builder of premium jet tenders, supplying superyachts, megayachts, and private owners globally. Williams tenders are known for their jet propulsion (no exposed propeller), high performance, and bespoke customization options that match the mother yacht's livery and specifications. Per Williams Jet Tenders official site and YachtBuyer.

## Lineup

**SportJet Series (petrol, high-performance):**
- **SportJet 280, 345, 435** -- Compact to mid-range models. 345 pre-owned from ballpark USD 50,000. Per Youboat.
- **SportJet 460** -- New for 2025: 170hp, top speed 48mph. Per Motor Boat & Yachting.
- **SportJet 520** -- New for 2025: 230hp supercharged, top speed 56mph. Flagship SportJet. Per Motor Boat & Yachting.
- **SportJet 750** -- Largest SportJet, for superyacht applications.

**DieselJet Series (diesel):**
- **DieselJet 505** -- Compact diesel tender.
- **DieselJet 625** -- New for 2025: 195hp diesel, 44mph, 11-person capacity. Per Motor Boat & Yachting.

**EvoJet Series (electric):**
- **EvoJet 70e** -- New for 2025: fully electric, 58.5kWh battery, 40mph, 13-person capacity. Per Motor Boat & Yachting.

## Recent Moves

Williams launched four new models for 2025, a significant product refresh. The SportJet 460 and 520 feature revised hulls and upgraded engines, with the 520 Limited Edition debuting at FLIBS 2024. The DieselJet 625 expands the diesel range. The EvoJet 70e marks Williams' entry into electric propulsion for environmentally conscious superyachts. Per Motor Boat & Yachting, 2025.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**DCH Marine is the exclusive Williams distributor for Asia-Pacific.** Led by Managing Director Adam Waters, DCH Marine has represented Williams since 2019, initially covering Hong Kong, Macau, and China. In February 2025, the partnership expanded to include Singapore, Philippines, and Taiwan. Per PressMare and Yacht Style, 2025.

DCH Marine also exclusively distributes Sunseeker and Saxdor in APAC. Per DCH Marine official site. For Thailand specifically, DCH Marine handles Williams sales and service, and has exhibited at regional boat shows.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Williams dominates the premium jet tender segment. Competitors include Highfield Boats (aluminium RIBs, more affordable), Brig (Ukraine, competitive pricing), and Northstar (Turkey, premium RIBs). Williams' differentiator is jet propulsion (safe, no propeller), performance credentials, and full customization to match the mother yacht's livery and specifications. No other brand offers the same bespoke superyacht integration.
