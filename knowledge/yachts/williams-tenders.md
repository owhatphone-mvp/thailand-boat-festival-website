## Identity

Williams Jet Tenders is a British, privately owned builder of jet-drive yacht tenders, based in Oxfordshire, UK. The world's best-known jet-tender brand: no exposed propeller (safer around swimmers), compact enough to fit yacht garages, and customisable to match the mother yacht. 10,000+ boats built (milestone in 2021).

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

- **DCH Marine** — exclusive Williams distributor for Hong Kong, Macau and mainland China (since 2019), expanded in Feb 2025 to Singapore, the Philippines and Taiwan.
- **Thailand:** not named in DCH's Williams territory (as of 2026). Cholamark Boat (Phuket) was appointed the first Thai Williams dealer back in 2012; current status unverified — tell clients to confirm via williamsjettenders.com or DCH Marine.

## Lineup (current models)

- **TurboJet MX 280MX / 320MX (NEW Aug 2026)** — 2.8m/9ft & 3.2m/10ft entry tenders, 60–90hp Rotax, ~36–39kt. ~EUR 20K–23.5K (≈USD 23K–27K). Replaces MiniJet 280.
- **TurboJet** — ~3m/10–11ft classic compact jet tenders (e.g. TurboJet 325).
- **SportJet** (345 up to 460 / 520) — 3.5–5.2m (11–17ft) tender-cum-ski boats; 520 = 230hp supercharged, ~56mph.
- **DieselJet** (incl. 565, 625) — up to 6.3m/21ft, Yanmar diesel; 625 carries 11 people. 565 available in SOLAS rescue spec.
- **EvoJet / EvoJet 70e** — 7.1m/23ft flagship (petrol or electric); 70e is fully electric, 58.5kWh, ~40mph, 13 people; for superyachts 40m+.
- Ballpark range: USD ~20K (TurboJet MX) to USD 200K+ (large diesel/electric spec); verify with dealer.

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)

- **Aug 2026** — TurboJet MX series launched (280MX, 320MX), priced ~20% below comparable jet tenders to win 40–70ft yacht owners.
- **Feb 2025** — DCH Marine Asia territory expanded (Singapore, Philippines, Taiwan).
- **2025 model year** — four new models: SportJet 460, SportJet 520, DieselJet 625, EvoJet 70e (electric).

## Positioning vs Competitors

Jet-drive specialist; RIB builders (Highfield, Brig) and custom tender yards (e.g. Castoldi, Pascoe) compete in parts of the market. Williams leads on compact, garage-ready jet tenders at volume.
