## Identity
Riva is the most storied name in Italian boating — founded 1842 on Lake Iseo and made iconic by Carlo Riva's mahogany Aquarama runabouts. Part of **Ferretti Group** since 2000, it now builds modern GRP boats and yachts from ~8m runabouts to the 40m 130' Bellissima; its historic yard is in Sarnico.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer
- **V Yachts Asia** has been the Thailand dealer for Riva (with Ferretti Yachts and Pershing) since 2021, based at Phuket Boat Lagoon with a Pattaya office; still active in 2026 — verify current coverage with Ferretti Group.
- Other Asian markets are served through Ferretti Group's Asia-Pacific network and local dealers.

## Lineup (current models)
- **Runabouts & small opens** — Iseo Super, El-Iseo (electric), Aquariva Special, Rivamare, Dolceriva (~8–16m / 27–53ft). Ballpark USD 0.5–2.5M.
- **Sport opens** — 56' Rivale Super, 58' Capri, 68' Diable, 76' Bahamas Super (~17–23m). Ballpark USD 2–7M.
- **Sportfly** — 66' Ribelle, 76' Perseo Super, 88' Folgore (~20–27m). Ballpark USD 3.5–10M.
- **Flybridge** — 82' Diva, **96' Argo Super**, 102' Corsaro Super (~25–31m). Ballpark USD 6–14M.
- **Superyachts** — 112' Dolcevita Super (~34m), 130' Bellissima (~40m). Ballpark USD 14–25M+.

## Recent Moves (2025–2026)
- **Sep–Oct 2026:** world premieres at Cannes of the **96' Argo Super** (28.79m, evolution of the 90' Argo, up to 28 knots, three or four guest cabins) and the **56' Rivale Super** (17.27m open, up to 38 knots); the Rivale Super was also shown at Genoa.
- **May 2026:** parent Ferretti Group named Stassi Anastassov CEO.
- **Jun 2025:** private world premiere at Sarnico of four models — **Iseo Super**, **Aquariva Special**, **Riva Cento** (limited edition based on the Rivamare 38') and **58' Capri**.

## Positioning vs Competitors
Unmatched heritage and brand recognition. Sanlorenzo competes on Italian craftsmanship; Azimut on flybridge range; sister brand Pershing is the pure-performance choice within the same group.
