## Identity

Riva is the most storied name in Italian yachting — founded in **1842** on Lake Iseo by Pietro Riva, and made iconic by **Carlo Riva** (1922-2017), whose mahogany Aquarama runabouts became symbols of la dolce vita. Acquired by **Ferretti Group** in May 2000, Riva builds modern GRP yachts across three categories — open, sportfly, and flybridge — from 10m to 40m. Per riva-yacht.com, Ferretti Group, Yacht Style.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

Riva unveiled four new yachts at a private world premiere in 2025. The **Riva Cento** is a limited-edition 38ft open celebrating the 100th Rivamare hull. The **Aquariva Special** restyles the brand's most iconic model with modern engineering. The **112' Dolcevita Super** replaces the 110' as the premier superyacht. The current range spans 15+ models. Per Ferretti Group, Yacht Style, riva-yacht.com, 2025.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**V Yachts Asia** is the exclusive Ferretti Group dealer for Thailand, covering Riva from **Phuket Boat Lagoon**. Riva's brand cachet is exceptionally strong in Asia — the name carries recognition even among non-boaters. **Simpson Marine** handles the broader APAC from Hong Kong. Flybridge models (Diva, Argo) are popular in Asia for cruising; the Aquariva Special serves as a day boat or tender. Per V Yachts Asia, Simpson Marine, Yacht Style, 2024-2026.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Riva's heritage is unmatched — 180+ years of continuous history. **Sanlorenzo** competes on Italian quality without the historical weight. **Azimut** offers a similar flybridge range. **Pershing** (sister brand) is the performance alternative. At the superyacht end (112' Dolcevita Super, 130' Bellissima), Riva competes with **Benetti** (Azimut|Benetti Group) and **Azimut Grande**.

## DO NOT

- Call Riva a "Ferretti" — it is Riva, part of Ferretti Group, but the brand identity stands alone
- Suggest current Riva yachts are built in mahogany — modern hulls are GRP with mahogany accents
- Compare Riva directly to Pershing — they serve completely different clients within the same group
- Name celebrity owners past or present — discretion is paramount
