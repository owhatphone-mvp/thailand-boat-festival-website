## Identity

Sanlorenzo is an Italian semi-custom yacht builder headquartered in Ameglia (Liguria), founded 1958. Under CEO **Massimo Perotti** (since 2005, formerly Azimut-Benetti), it grew from EUR 42M to EUR 930M revenue — the "Haute Couture" of yachting. Listed on Milan Stock Exchange (Euronext STAR, ticker: SL) since December 2018. Produces ~65 yachts/year across four Italian shipyards, preserving exclusivity through limited volume. Per sanlorenzoyacht.com, Yacht Style, Superyacht Times.

## Lineup (current models, 2024-2026)

**Yachts (composite/GRP):**
- **SL** — semi-displacement, the core range. SL78 to SL106 Asymmetric (24–32m / 78–106ft). Ballpark USD 3–10M.
- **SD** — crossover, design-forward with asymmetric layouts. SD96 to SD132 (28.9–40.7m / 95–132ft). SD132 launched Dec 2024. Ballpark USD 5–18M.
- **SP** — planing performance. SP110, SP130 (33–40m / 108–130ft). SP110 won the Compasso d'Oro 2024, Italy's highest design honour. 25-28 knots. Ballpark USD 7–15M.
- **SX** — modern explorer crossover. SX88 to SX112 (26.7–34m / 88–112ft). Strong resale momentum. Ballpark USD 6.5–15M.
- **SHE (new 2025)** — vintage-inspired 26m/84ft standalone model, classic yacht aesthetic with modern engineering.

**Superyachts (steel/aluminium):**
- **44 Alloy** — 44.5m/146ft fast displacement aluminium. 480GT, 21 knots. Ballpark USD 26-30M.
- **Steel (50/52/62)** — full displacement. 50Steel "Almax" is world's first bio-methanol fuel-cell superyacht. Ballpark USD 30–50M+.
- **X-Space** — newest line. 44 and 50 X-Space with solar capability. Fourth hull contracted.

Per sanlorenzoyacht.com, BellaYacht, YachtBuyer, Superyacht Times, 2024-2026.

## Recent Moves (2024-2026)

FY2024 revenue EUR 930.4M (+10.7%). Backlog EUR 1.44B (93% sold, H1 2025). SD132 (40.7m) launched December 2024. SHE concept debuted 2025. SP110 won Compasso d'Oro 2024. 50Steel's bio-methanol fuel cell = green-technology leader. Bluegame (acquired 2018) produces sport-yachts (13–23m). Per sanlorenzoyacht.com, Yacht Harbour, Superyacht Times.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer

**Simpson Marine** (founded 1984, HK-based) is Sanlorenzo's exclusive APAC dealer network, with 11 offices across Hong Kong (HQ), Singapore, Phuket, Sydney, Jakarta, and other Asian cities. In March 2024, Sanlorenzo Asia Pacific formalised a **strategic partnership with Vrit Yongsakul (Boat Lagoon Yachting)** for Thailand, operating as "Sanlorenzo Thailand" through Simpson Marine's network. Over 100 Sanlorenzo yachts delivered across APAC. Per apac.sanlorenzoyacht.com, Yacht Style, Superyacht Times.

## Positioning vs Competitors

Sanlorenzo sits between production builders (Azimut, Princess) and full-custom yards (Lürssen, Feadship). "Made-to-measure" means each yacht is individually tailored — more customization than production, faster delivery than full-custom. vs. Azimut: more exclusive, lower volume, higher price per foot. vs. Benetti: competes at the smaller end, but Benetti goes 80m+. vs. Heesen: closest rival in 35-55m semi-custom; Heesen is Dutch and aluminium-specialist, Sanlorenzo offers Italian design.

## DO NOT

- State that Sanlorenzo owns Perini Navi — they tried to acquire it but were outbid by The Italian Sea Group (TISG) in 2022
- Name the CEO as "Massimo Perretti" — correct spelling is **Perotti**
- Quote definitive pricing — Sanlorenzo's semi-custom approach means every build is priced differently
- Confuse Ameglia (corporate HQ) with La Spezia (production shipyard) — both are correct in different contexts
