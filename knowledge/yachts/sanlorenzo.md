## Identity
Italian semi-custom ("made-to-measure") yacht and superyacht builder, founded 1958, headquartered in Ameglia (La Spezia). Listed on Euronext Milan (STAR) since 2019 and led by Massimo Perotti. The group also owns Bluegame, Nautor Swan and Asia-Pacific dealer Simpson Marine.

## Asian Market & Thai Dealer
- **Simpson Marine** (Hong Kong HQ, ~12 offices across Asia) is Sanlorenzo's Asia-Pacific network and has been majority-owned by Sanlorenzo since 2024.
- **Sanlorenzo Thailand** (Phuket), launched August 2025 with a Thai yachting partner, handles exclusive sales of Sanlorenzo and Bluegame in Thailand (as of 2026).
- Over 100 Sanlorenzo yachts delivered across APAC.

## Lineup (current models)
- **SL** (planing flybridge, incl. asymmetric) — ~24–36m / 78–120ft. Ballpark USD 3–15M.
- **SD** (semi-displacement) — SD90 to SD132 (~28–40m / 90–132ft). Ballpark USD 5–18M.
- **SX** (crossover) — ~23–34m / 76–112ft. Ballpark USD 5–15M.
- **SP** (sport) — SP110 (~34m / 110ft). Ballpark USD 10–14M.
- **Heritage** — **SHE** (25.5m / 84ft), 1960s-inspired.
- **Superyachts** — Alloy, Steel and X-Space lines (~44–62m / 145–203ft). Ballpark USD 25–60M+.
- **Bluegame** (sister brand) — BG/BGX/BGM models ~13–23m, including the new BG64 (20m).

## Positioning vs Competitors
Sits between production builders (Azimut, Princess, Ferretti) and full-custom yards: each yacht is tailored, with limited annual volume supporting exclusivity and resale.
