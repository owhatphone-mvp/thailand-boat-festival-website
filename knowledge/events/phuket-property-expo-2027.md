# Phuket Property Expo 2027 (งานอสังหาภูเก็ต 2027)

> **File path:** `knowledge/events/phuket-property-expo-2027.md`

> **Last updated:** 2026-10-26
> **Source:** propertyexpophuket.com + expo Facebook page

## Key facts
- 1st edition. The luxury property show inside Thailand Boat Festival 2027.
- 14–17 January 2027, 13:00–19:00 daily (the festival docks are open 14:00–21:00).
- White House hall (air-conditioned), Boat Lagoon Marina — steps from the docks; ~20 min from the airport, 9 km from Phuket Town.
- Entry by registration, no ticket. One registration, through either event, admits to both. Early Bird closes 30 November 2026. Visitors: https://thailandboatfestival.com/property/earlybird · details: https://thailandboatfestival.com/property (also propertyexpophuket.com).
- Organised by M Vision PCL, co-organised with Dr. Phongthon Tharachai (PP Richer is Better).

## Confirmed in the hall so far (announced on the expo's Facebook page)
- **Garden Atlas Bayview** — 15 luxury villas on the Cape Panwa hillside with views across three bays (Ao Makham, the Sri Panwa coast, Ao Yon–Khao Khad). Booth WH02.
- **Glenalmond Phuket** — international school from Scotland, British curriculum; Early Years and Primary now, Years 7–12 opening 2027. Booth WH12. Useful for families planning a move to Phuket.
- **Thai Seaplane** — Thai Seaplane Lounge inside the White House.
More exhibitors are announced as they confirm. Never name a developer that is not on this list.

## What visitors will find
Luxury villas, branded residences, condominiums and new developments from their developers; agencies (resale, long-term leases); advisers for foreign buyers. Every booth has a table and seats to sit down with the developer.

## Hall and booths (for exhibitors)
- 52 booth modules of 3 × 3 m: 26 along the walls and 26 on a centre island; one visitor loop passes every booth; aisles 3.5 m; registration under the canopy outside.
- Booth types: wall (18), corner open two sides (12), island end — premium, open two sides, first in view from the entrance (4), island (18), double 6 × 3 m (two modules).
- Standard booth: partition walls, lit name board, poster wall, counter, table and chairs (concept).
- Ways to take part: booth module, island-end booth, title partner ("presented by", one per edition). All **rates on request** — never quote a price; take name, company/project, email/phone and booth interest. Exhibitor pack is also requested via the form on /property.
- No lounge and no stage in the hall (older materials mentioning "Property Talks" or a lounge are outdated).

## Why exhibit (talking points)
- The audience: festival visitors come to a marina in January, high season, when international buyers are already on the island.
- Market (published): world's 5th-largest branded-residence market, ~US$14bn pipeline (C9 Hotelworks, 2026); 23 condo launches in 2025 (Colliers); 16 villa projects H1 2026 (CBRE); average price THB 12.8M (Nation, Sep 2026).

## DO NOT
- Say "PPX" or "first in Thailand"; mention a lounge/stage/Property Talks; quote booth rates; give foreign-ownership legal advice.
