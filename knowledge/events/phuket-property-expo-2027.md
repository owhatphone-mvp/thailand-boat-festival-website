# Phuket Property Expo 2027 (งานอสังหาภูเก็ต 2027)

> **Last updated:** 2026-10-27 · **Source:** propertyexpophuket.com (= thailandboatfestival.com/property, EN + TH) + expo Facebook page

## Key facts
- 1st edition. The luxury property show inside Thailand Boat Festival 2027.
- 14–17 January 2027, 13:00–19:00 daily (festival docks 14:00–21:00).
- White House hall (air-conditioned, 50 × 20 m), Boat Lagoon Marina, steps from the docks; 9 km from Phuket Town, ~20 min from the airport.
- Entry by registration, no ticket. One registration, through either event, admits to both. Early Bird closes 30 November 2026: https://thailandboatfestival.com/property/earlybird
- Site: https://thailandboatfestival.com/property (Thai: /property/th) · 3D walkthrough of the hall: https://thailandboatfestival.com/property/3d/ · Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61594883987623
- Organised by M Vision PCL, co-organised with Dr. Phongthon Tharachai (PP Richer is Better).
- Property agents/brokers: one registration covers both events: https://thailandboatfestival.com/broker

## Confirmed in the hall so far (announced on the expo's Facebook page)
- **Garden Atlas Bayview**: 15 luxury villas on the Cape Panwa hillside, views across three bays. Booth WH02.
- **Glenalmond Phuket**: international school from Scotland, British curriculum; Early Years and Primary now, Years 7–12 from 2027. Booth WH12. Good for families moving to Phuket.
- **Thai Seaplane**: Thai Seaplane Lounge inside the White House.
More are announced as they confirm. Never name a developer not on this list.

## What visitors will find
Luxury villas, branded residences, condominiums and new launches from their developers; agencies (resale, long-term leases); advisers for foreign buyers. Every booth has a table and seats to sit with the developer.

## Hall and booths (for exhibitors)
- 52 modules of 3 × 3 m: 26 on the walls, 26 on a centre island; 3.5 m aisles, one loop passes every booth; registration under the canopy outside.
- Types: wall (18), corner, open two sides (12), island end, premium, first in view from the entrance (4), island (18), double 6 × 3 m.
- Standard booth: white partitions, lit name board, poster wall, counter, table with covered chairs (concept; fit-out to be confirmed).
- Ways to take part: booth module, island-end booth, title partner ("presented by", one per edition). All **rates on request**: never quote a price; take name, company/project, email/phone and booth interest.

## Why exhibit
- Visitors come to a marina in January, high season, when international buyers are on the island.
- Market: world's 5th-largest branded-residence market, ~US$14bn pipeline (C9 Hotelworks, 2026); 23 condo launches in 2025 (Colliers); 16 villa projects H1 2026 (CBRE); average price THB 12.8M (Nation, Sep 2026).

## DO NOT
- Say "PPX" or "first in Thailand"; quote booth rates; give foreign-ownership legal advice.
- Mention a property lounge, stage or "Property Talks" (dropped; the Thai Seaplane Lounge is the only lounge).
- Present the 3D model as a sales plan: it is a design concept.
