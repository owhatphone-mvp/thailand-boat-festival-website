## Summary (one paragraph)

Asia's regatta calendar runs in parallel with the yacht show circuit, creating touchpoints where yacht owners, crew, and industry converge. The centrepiece is the Phuket King's Cup Regatta — Asia's oldest and most prestigious sailing event, held annually since 1987. Per the official King's Cup website and Sunsail, the regatta draws competitors from 20+ countries and has grown from 15 yachts at founding to 100+ entries. Beyond Phuket, the calendar includes Hong Kong Race Week, the biennial Hong Kong to Vietnam Race, and regional events. For TBF, regattas represent both a marketing opportunity (overlapping audiences of wealthy yacht owners) and a logistical factor (many Phuket-based owners are in the racing community).

## Relevance to TBF / Sand

**Phuket King's Cup Regatta — The Key Connection:**
- Runs late November through early December — weeks before TBF's January dates.
- Many Phuket-based yacht owners participate in or attend the King's Cup, creating natural audience overlap with TBF.
- Per Sunsail and Burgess Yachts, "Asia's biggest and most prestigious regatta," drawing a social crowd beyond the racing fleet.
- Founded 1987 to honour King Bhumibol Adulyadej's birthday. Royal patronage gives unique prestige.

**How regattas connect to the yacht market:**
- **Owners bring boats:** Many sail from HK, Singapore, Malaysia, or Australia to Phuket. Boats stay berthed for winter — same season as TBF.
- **Networking:** Regatta parties and prize ceremonies create informal networking among owners, brokers, captains. Many TBF visitors attended the King's Cup weeks earlier.
- **Upgrade cycle:** Racing owners upgrade every 5–10 years. Regatta comparisons spark interest. TBF, weeks later, is the natural place to explore.
- **Charter/crew:** Regattas attract crews and charter operators — referral sources for sales.

**Broader context:**
- HK Race Week and HK to Vietnam Race draw a more racing-focused demographic with corporate sponsorship, attracting HK/Chinese owners who may also attend TBF.
- The biennial HK to Vietnam Race (October 2025) finishes in Nha Trang, connecting HK and Vietnamese yachting communities.
- No significant Bali Regatta confirmed for 2025–2026.

## DO NOT

- Say TBF is "part of" the King's Cup — they are independent events
- Confuse regatta visitors (competitive sailors) with boat show visitors (lifestyle buyers)
- Imply racing yachts are the same as motor yachts at TBF — most King's Cup entries are sailboats
- Quote specific race results or boat names without context
