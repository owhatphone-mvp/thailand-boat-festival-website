# TBF 2027 Docks & Boat-Size Fit (official floor plan)
Finger berths are double: 2 boats share a bay. Beam per boat ~ bay/2 - 0.5 m. Catamarans need a whole bay or side-tie. Monohulls over ~16.4 m need side-tie (MC or G). Total on-water: 50+. Sales team allocates berths; never promise one, never quote prices.

| Dock | Type | Berth length m | Bay width m | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MC01 / MC02 | side-tie quay | ~25 / ~28.9 | open | 20-28 m yachts, big cats |
| G01 | side-tie quay | 3 large spaces (length tbc) | open | 18-25 m yachts |
| G02 | short quay | 20.4 frontage | - | tenders, water toys |
| F south | fingers | 15.8, 16.4 x3 | 12.8 x3, 9.1 | 14-16 m (beam <= ~5.9), or 1 cat per 12.8 bay |
| E north | fingers | 15.8, 16.4 x2, 14.6, 13.4 | 14, 13.7, 12.1, 10.3 x2 | 13-16 m |
| E south | fingers | 13.1, 13.4 x4, 7.9 | 10.9 | 11-13 m (beam <= ~4.9) |
| F north | fingers | 13.1, 13.4 x3, 10.6 | 11.2, 9.7, 10.9, 9.7, 7 | 9-13 m |
| C north | fingers | 8.5-9.0 | 4.8-8.8 | up to ~9 m |
| C south | fingers | 6.8, 8.5, 12.2 x2 | 7 x3, 7.4, 11.5 | 7-12 m |

Examples (LOA x beam x draft, m):
- Sunseeker 90 Ocean 27.10 x 7.16 x 1.94 -> MC02 only; move in 10-12 Jan midday HW, leave 20-22 Jan
- Sanlorenzo SL86A 26.6 x 6.35 x 1.96 -> MC02 only; same timing
- Princess Y72 21.79 x 5.51 x 1.73 -> MC01 or G01
- Azimut Fly 60 18.25 x 5.05 x 1.46 -> G01/MC; 16.4 m finger only with marina OK for overhang
- Leopard 46 cat 14.02 x 7.34 x 1.63 / Lagoon 46 cat 14.5 x 7.96 x 1.37 -> whole 12.8 m bay on F south, or MC01

For other boats: ask model, LOA, beam, max draft, number of boats, preferred arrival day -> pass to sales team. Exhibitors from the last edition get priority berth selection during early bird until 6 Nov 2026.
