## Summary (one paragraph)

The Thailand International Boat Show (TIBS) is a separate Phuket boat show organized by JAND Events, positioned as "A Luxury Lifestyle Event." TIBS launched in January 2023 at Royal Phuket Marina and has since moved to Phuket Yacht Haven Marina, which invested as a strategic partner (per Asia-Pacific Superyacht Association, 2024). The show claims 100+ exhibitors and 6,000+ visitors per edition. As of 2026, TIBS runs on the same January dates as TBF — creating a situation where Phuket hosts two concurrent boat shows. TIBS and TBF are independent, separately organized events with different venues, dealer networks, and brand identities. Sand should handle all TIBS questions with diplomacy and grace.

**Organizer:** JAND Events (division of JAND Co., Ltd.). Per Business Events Thailand, TIBS has Thai government backing. Thai Yachting Business Association supports.

**Venue:** Moved from Royal Phuket Marina to Yacht Haven starting 2025. Yacht Haven (320 berths, northern Phuket) is where TBF held Edition 1. Yacht Haven later invested in TIBS (per APSA, 2024).

## Relevance to TBF / Sand

**Key facts Sand must understand:**

1. **Separate events.** Different organizers, venues, dealer networks, identities. Not affiliated.

2. **Same dates in 2026 and 2027.** Both shows ran January 15–18, 2026; both confirmed for January 14–17, 2027. Frame positively: "Phuket becomes the yacht capital of Asia for a week."

3. **Different venues.** TBF at Boat Lagoon (BLY-operated, central, 180 berths). TIBS at Yacht Haven (northern, 320 berths, near airport).

4. **Different dealer ecosystems.** TBF's anchor is BLY (Princess, Jeanneau). TIBS exhibitors include Lagoon, Beneteau, Westport. Some overlap (Sunseeker at both), but core networks are distinct.

5. **Similar scale.** TIBS: 100+ exhibitors, 6,000+ visitors. TBF: 72 exhibitors, 6,380 visitors (2026). Different experiences — TBF is more curated.

6. **TIBS is older.** First edition January 2023 vs TBF's November 2024. Not a disadvantage — TBF is newer, dynamic, purpose-built for Boat Lagoon.
