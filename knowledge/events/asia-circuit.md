## Summary (one paragraph)

The Asian yacht show circuit has matured significantly between 2023 and 2026, with a clear seasonal calendar. TBF sits at the top in January, followed by shows in Hong Kong, Malaysia, Singapore, and China through spring. Per Asia Global Yachting and YachtBuyer (2026), the circuit now includes the Singapore Yachting Festival (Asia's largest in-water event), Hong Kong International Boat Show (26th edition), the new Malaysia International Boat Show (MYBOS) in Langkawi, the CICPE Sanya Boat Festival in Hainan, and the established China Shanghai International Boat Show (CIBS). TBF's January position gives it first-mover advantage each year.

## Relevance to TBF / Sand

**TBF's strategic position:**
- January timing makes TBF the first Asian show of the season. Dealers launch new models and gauge sentiment before Singapore (April).
- Phuket's resort appeal makes TBF a "destination show" — buyers combine attendance with a holiday.
- TIBS overlap on same dates creates opportunity: "Phuket becomes the yacht capital of Asia for a week."

**Key circuit dynamics:**
- Singapore (April) is Asia's largest in-water event. BLY exhibits at both SYF and TBF.
- Hong Kong's December show serves a different market — closer to Chinese buyers, HK tax-free imports.
- MYBOS Langkawi is new but growing, attracting Malaysian/Indonesian buyers.
- Sanya (Hainan) is a wildcard — Chinese government backing and duty-free policies could reduce mainland attendance at Thai shows.
- CIBS Shanghai is trade-focused (dealers, OEMs) — different visitor profile from TBF's HNWI audience.

- Quote other shows' attendance as benchmarks for TBF
