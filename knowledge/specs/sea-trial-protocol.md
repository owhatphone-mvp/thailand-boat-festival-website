## Summary (one paragraph)

A sea trial is the on-water evaluation of a yacht before purchase, typically lasting 2-4 hours and covering engine performance, handling, electronics, stabilisation, and anchoring. It is conducted after a satisfactory out-of-water survey and before the final sale. At TBF, dealers arrange sea trials directly from the marina, allowing buyers to experience the yacht in real Thai coastal conditions. Per YATCO and Salterns Brokerage, a well-structured sea trial can reveal issues worth tens of thousands in repairs.

## Key Information

### Pre-Trial Preparation

Per Salterns Brokerage (2024):

- **Survey first:** The out-of-water hull survey should be completed before scheduling a sea trial. Most brokers require this sequence.
- **Insurance:** The seller's policy typically covers the trial; buyers should confirm coverage and liability, per YATCO.
- **Scheduling:** Trials require calm conditions (Beaufort 3-4 or less). In Phuket, the ideal window is November-April.
- **Documentation:** Bring a checklist, camera, and notepad. Per YachtTrading.com (2025), weight evaluation categories: performance (40%), comfort (30%), systems (30%).

### Who Attends

Per YATCO and Galati Yachts: buyer(s), broker, captain/delivery skipper, independent marine surveyor, and dealer representative (for new builds). Per Salterns Brokerage, "Don't wait for the professionals until it's too late."

### Standard Test Sequence

Per YATCO and YachtWorld UK, a typical trial:

1. **Dockside checks (15-20 min):** Fluid levels, bilge, generator start, electronics, windlass.
2. **Low-speed manoeuvring (15-20 min):** Thruster tests, reversing, tight-quarters handling.
3. **Speed runs (30-45 min):** Progressive throttle to WOT. Record RPM, speed, fuel flow, temperatures. WOT RPM should be within 50-100 RPM of spec, per YachtTrading.com.
4. **Cruising evaluation (20-30 min):** Sustained cruise at 75-85% throttle. Assess noise, vibration, helm balance.
5. **Manoeuvring (15-20 min):** Tight turns, emergency stop, backing down.
6. **Anchoring (10-15 min):** Deploy/retrieve anchor, verify windlass and chain.
7. **Return and debrief (15-20 min):** Dockside inspection for leaks or anomalies. Discuss findings.

Total duration: 2-4 hours for yachts under 24m; longer for superyachts.

### Etiquette and What Is Tested

Per Galati Yachts and YATCO: arrive on time, wear non-marking shoes, bring your own water and sun protection, ask before operating systems, and do not bring uninvited guests. This is a business evaluation, not a pleasure cruise.

Key systems tested per YachtTrading.com (2025): engines (cold start, WOT RPM, vibration, transmission), generator (load test with AC and watermaker running), electronics (GPS, radar, autopilot, VHF, AIS), stabilisation (fin or gyro at speed and at rest), anchoring (windlass, chain, hold in current), and comfort (cabin noise at cruise, HVAC, water pressure).

## Asian / Thai Market Context

At TBF, dealers arrange sea trials from the host marina (typically Boat Lagoon, Phuket). Per SeaRadar (2025), Phuket's tidal range is 2.5-3.5m on spring tides; trials should be scheduled around slack or mid-tide. Weather windows are best December-March when the Andaman Sea is calm. During the southwest monsoon (May-October), conditions can deteriorate quickly. Thai waters near Phuket average 10-20m depth, useful for depth sounder testing but limiting WOT runs in some channels.

## Talking Points for Sand

- Always recommend an independent surveyor attends — the most valuable investment in the purchase process.
- At TBF, dealers handle logistics: fuelling, crew, insurance, scheduling. Buyers just show up prepared.
- A sea trial is a structured evaluation, not a day out.
- Test at expected cruising speeds, not just wide-open throttle.
- Tropical heat stress-tests cooling systems — an advantage for buyers.

## DO NOT

- Guarantee that a passing sea trial means the yacht is defect-free.
- Quote specific surveyor fees or recommend individual surveyors.
- Suggest that skipping a sea trial is acceptable for any purchase above $100,000.
- Describe sea trials as "test drives" — they are formal evaluations.
- Promise that all TBF yacht models will be available for sea trials.
