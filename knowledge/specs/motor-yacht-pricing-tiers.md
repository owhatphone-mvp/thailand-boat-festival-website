## Summary (one paragraph)

Motor yacht pricing spans from approximately $300,000 for a compact production cruiser to over $100 million for a custom superyacht. Prices depend primarily on LOA, builder pedigree, and fit-out level. Thailand's 2004 removal of import duty (zero customs/excise tax) plus 7% VAT on CIF value makes it one of Asia's most cost-effective countries to import a yacht. Per Sail-World, 18 February 2004, qualifying categories include sailboats, motorboats, inflatables, and other pleasure craft. New yachts lose 10-20% in year one and 40-50% by year five, per YATCO.

## Key Information

### Tier 1 — Entry Level: 10-15m (33-50ft) — Ballpark $300K-$1.5M

Production builders dominate. Per ECYS (2025), an Axopar 38 ranges from approximately $470K (base) to $670K (fully optioned). The Axopar 45 XC lists near $989K. Per Beneteau's Gran Turismo range (2025), a GT40 starts around $350-450K and a GT50 approaches $650-750K. Jeanneau's Merry Fisher and Cap Camarat lines occupy a similar bracket. Used examples (3-5 years) can be 30-40% below new.

### Tier 2 — Mid-Range: 15-24m (50-80ft) — Ballpark $1.5M-$5M

Per YachtTrading.com (2026), a 60-foot yacht typically costs $800K-$3M new. The Ferretti Yachts 720 (22m) lists new at approximately $4.2-4.7M, per YachtWorld (2025). Azimut's Fly and Magellano ranges, Princess V-Class, and Sunseeker Manhattan models populate this tier. UK-built yachts tend to hold value slightly better than Italian counterparts.

### Tier 3 — Large: 24-35m (80-115ft) — Ballpark $5M-$15M

Per Princess Yachts Monaco (2025), the Y95 (29m) lists at approximately $8.5M+ excluding VAT. The Azimut Grande 30M sits in the $10-13M range. Sunseeker 90+ models and Ferretti Custom Line round out this tier. These yachts feature full-beam master suites, crew quarters, and ocean-crossing capability.

### Tier 4 — Superyacht: 35m+ (115ft+) — Ballpark $15M-$100M+

Per Boat International and Burgess (2025), a 37m Heesen lists around $18-22M; a 55m Heesen near $30-35M. Feadship, Lurssen, and Benetti custom builds at 60m+ routinely exceed $60-100M. Lurssen's largest builds surpass $160M, per Edmiston (2025).

### Catamaran Tier (Separate)

Per sellyourboat.io (2026), catamarans range from roughly $175K (used) to $18M+ (premium). Lagoon 42: ~$500-600K new. Lagoon 51: ~$1.3-1.6M. Lagoon 65: ~$2.5-3M+. Sunreef 60 Power (2026): ~$7.5M, per YachtWorld. Fountaine Pajot motor yachts start around $800K-$1.2M.

### New vs Used Differentials

Per YATCO, new yachts lose 10-20% in year one, 30-40% by year three, and 40-50% by year five. A well-maintained 5-year-old yacht may be priced at half its original new price.

## Asian / Thai Market Context

Thailand's zero import duty since February 2004 gives buyers a major cost advantage over neighbours charging 20-60% in combined duties. Per Sail-World (2004), the exemption covers motorboats, sailboats, inflatables, and other pleasure craft. Only 7% VAT applies on CIF value. Per Forbes & Partners (2025), this has made Phuket a regional hub for yacht ownership and attracted international dealers. VAT-exempt temporary import permits have time limits (typically 6 months, extendable).

## Talking Points for Sand

- All pricing is ballpark USD only; actual prices depend on specification and market conditions.
- The 15-24m tier is the "sweet spot" for most TBF visitors: entertaining space without a full crew.
- Catamarans are increasingly popular in Thailand due to shallow draft and stable platform.
- Thailand's 0% import duty applies to hull and equipment; engines may have separate classifications.
- Used yachts offer excellent value, especially professionally maintained ex-charter vessels.
