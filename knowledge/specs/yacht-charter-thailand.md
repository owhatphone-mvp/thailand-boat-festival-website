## Summary (one paragraph)

Thailand offers bareboat, crewed, and day charter experiences across spectacular cruising grounds including Phang Nga Bay, Phi Phi Islands, and the Similan archipelago. Foreign-flagged yachts over 24m can obtain a Thai Charter License to operate commercially; Thai-flagged vessels require 70% Thai ownership. Per Asia Global Yachting and TYBA (2024-2025), the charter license threshold was lowered from 30m to 24m, potentially doubling eligible foreign yachts. Season runs November through April, peak December to February.

## Key Information

### Charter Types

- **Bareboat** (no crew): Charterer acts as skipper. Per Faraway Yachting (2025), requires a valid captain's license (RYA Day Skipper minimum), VHF certificate, and two weeks' experience. Minimum: 2-3 nights low season, 7 days high season.
- **Crewed:** Most popular for visitors. Skipper-only to full crew with chef and deckhands.
- **Day charter:** Per private-yacht.com (2025), sunset cruises from ~$570; full-day from ~$640 (30ft speedboat, 6 guests). Crewed motor yacht day charters: $1,500-$5,000+.

### Thai Charter Regulations

Per Herrera & Partners (October 2025) and Asia Global Yachting (2025):

- **Thai-flagged:** Company must have 70%+ capital held by Thai nationals. Vessels over 60 GT need additional licences.
- **Foreign-flagged:** Require Thai Charter License. Per Yacht Style (2024), threshold reduced from 30m to 24m LOA. Max 12 passengers, valid one year. Unlicensed operations risk fines to 500,000 THB or detention. 7% VAT and 5% CIT apply on charter fees, per Asia-Pacific Superyachts (2025).
- **Crew:** STCW certifications and work permits required. Thai crew not mandatory. Crew visas currently 30 days, plans to extend to 30 months.

### Popular Charter Areas

Per Boatbookings and Asia Global Yachting (2025-2026):

- **Phang Nga Bay:** Most iconic — limestone karsts, sea caves, emerald lagoons. Best by yacht for shallow-draft lagoon access.
- **Phi Phi Islands:** Maya Bay, clear waters, snorkelling. Overnight anchoring recommended.
- **Similan Islands:** Marine park open mid-October to mid-May. Pristine diving, liveaboard or private yacht only.
- **Krabi/Railay Beach:** Limestone cliffs, secluded beaches. Often combined with Phi Phi and Phang Nga.
- **Surin and Butang Islands:** Less-visited alternatives for experienced cruisers.

### Season and Rates

Per 12 Knots, Faraway Yachting, and private-yacht.com (2025-2026):

- **Season:** November-April. Peak December-February (northeast trades, calm Andaman seas).
- **Ballpark rates (USD):** Day (speedboat): $570-$1,500. Day (crewed motor yacht 20m+): $1,500-$5,000+. Weekly bareboat (catamaran 40-45ft): $2,000-$4,500. Weekly crewed (24m+): $15,000-$50,000+. Per 12 Knots (2025), crewed from ~$1,591/week.
- Rates 30-50% lower than Mediterranean or Caribbean, per Faraway Yachting.

### Key Operators

Established operators from Phuket include Faraway Yachting (30+ years), 12 Knots, Boatbookings, and boutique operators. TBF connects visitors with charter operators alongside purchase opportunities.

## Asian / Thai Market Context

Thailand's charter market benefits from world-class cruising grounds, affordable provisioning, and zero import duty on yachts. Per Asia Global Yachting (2026), the 24m license threshold change (championed by TYBA) should attract more foreign-flagged yachts. Leasing structures allow companies with 50%+ foreign shareholding to operate, per Herrera & Partners (2025). The 70% Thai ownership requirement for Thai-flagged vessels remains a barrier for foreign investors.

## Talking Points for Sand

- Charter is an excellent way for TBF visitors to experience Thai waters before a purchase.
- Bareboat charter requires genuine qualifications — not just signing a form.
- Crewed charters are the most stress-free option.
- Similan Islands are only accessible during charter season (closed in monsoon).
- Chartering on a foreign-flagged yacht without a valid Thai Charter License is illegal.

## DO NOT

- Recommend specific charter companies as the "best."
- Quote definitive prices — rates vary enormously by vessel, season, and inclusions.
- Suggest bareboat chartering suits beginners without qualifications.
- State that the license process is "easy" — it involves regulatory compliance.
- Mention owner names or identify specific vessels.
