## Summary (one paragraph)

Berthing costs across Asia vary enormously by location and vessel size. Phuket offers the best value among premium destinations at roughly THB 25-70/ft/day, while Singapore and Hong Kong are significantly more expensive. Per Kitalent (2025), berth capacity in Phuket is projected to reach 95% utilisation, making forward planning essential. Annual berthing in Phuket for a 50ft motor yacht runs $8,000-$16,000, whereas Singapore or Hong Kong can cost $25,000-$50,000+ including membership debentures.

## Key Information

### Phuket Marinas (Thailand)

Per Phuket Boat Lagoon published rates (November 2025) and Forbes & Partners (2025):

- **Phuket Boat Lagoon** (300+ berths): Monohull <70ft — Daily THB 70/ft (~$2.0/ft), Monthly THB 1,300/ft (~$37/ft). Catamaran <70ft — Daily THB 55/ft, Monthly THB 920/ft. Utilities at 20% of berth rate. Vessels 72ft+ quoted individually.
- **Yacht Haven Marina** (270+ berths): Comparable to Boat Lagoon. Per TYBA (2024), similar THB/ft pricing with annual discounts of 10-15%.
- **Ao Po Grand Marina** (230 berths): Per Forbes & Partners (2025), approximately THB 25-40/ft/day — one of the more affordable options.
- **Royal Phuket Marina** (5 Gold Anchor certified, only one in Thailand): Per Forbes & Partners (2025), THB 25-40/ft/day, monthly costs THB 47,500 to 95,000+ depending on size.

**Ballpark annual cost (50ft motor yacht): $8,000-$16,000.**

### Singapore

Per RSYC, SG Marine Guide, and ONE15 Marina (2024-2025):

- **ONE15 Marina Sentosa Cove** (270 berths, superyacht-capable): Rates on request. Estimated S$15-30/ft/day for members. Membership involves initiation fees and monthly dues.
- **Raffles Marina:** Similar range to ONE15, tiered member/non-member rates.
- **RSYC:** Wet berth maintenance ~S$6/ft/month for members. Dry berths S$450-$1,000/month.

**Ballpark annual cost (50ft): $25,000-$45,000 including membership.**

### Hong Kong

Per Simpson Marine, RHKYC, and BoatMarket HK (2025):

- **Royal Hong Kong Yacht Club:** Causeway Bay mooring ~HK$4,420/month for 8-11m vessels. Debentures HK$90,000 to HK$8,000,000+, per Simpson Marine. 5-year waiting list.
- **Gold Coast Yacht & Country Club** (200 berths): Debenture-based membership.
- **Hebe Haven** and **Aberdeen Marina Club:** Also require membership/debenture.

**Ballpark annual cost (50ft): $30,000-$55,000+ (heavily membership-dependent).**

### Malaysia

Per Noonsite, Cruisers Forum, and International Living (2024-2025):

- **Straits Quay Marina** (Penang): Monthly rates approximately $300-$600 for vessels under 50ft.
- **Telaga Harbour** (Langkawi): 27-acre basin, monthly approximately $300-$500. Langkawi is duty-free.
- **Rebak Island Resort** (Langkawi): Approximately 350 MYR (~$80)/month for boats under 30ft, scaled rates for larger vessels.

**Ballpark annual cost (50ft): $4,000-$8,000.**

### Indonesia

Per Yacht Style, MegaYacht News, and YachtCharterFleet (2025):

- **Bali Benoa Marina** (under construction, Phase 1 Q4 2025): Per MegaYacht News (July 2025), Indonesia's first international full-service marina. 180 berths when complete. Pricing not yet published.
- **Batavia Marina** (Jakarta): Estimated $200-$400/month for mid-size vessels.

**Ballpark annual cost (50ft): $3,000-$6,000 (estimated; limited published data).**

## Asian / Thai Market Context

Per Kitalent (2025), Phuket's yacht boom has pushed berth utilisation to 95%, meaning new owners may face waiting lists — especially for catamaran berths. Phuket costs are 50-70% lower than Singapore and Hong Kong. Langkawi's duty-free status adds value for provisioning. Hong Kong's debenture system pushes owners toward alternative ports.

## Talking Points for Sand

- All rates are ballpark USD; actual charges are in local currencies and subject to change.
- Annual contracts typically offer 10-20% savings over monthly rates.
- Catamaran berths cost more and are harder to find — book well in advance.
- Phuket offers the best combination of value, infrastructure, and cruising grounds in Asia.
- TBF host marina berth availability is a selling point — visitors can discuss long-term berthing directly.

## DO NOT

- Quote exact current rates as definitive — always frame as ballpark.
- Guarantee berth availability at any specific marina.
- Compare marinas disparagingly.
- Include private or members-only rate sheets.
- Suggest berthing costs are negotiable (some are fixed, some promotional).
