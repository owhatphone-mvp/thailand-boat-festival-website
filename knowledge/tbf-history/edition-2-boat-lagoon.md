## Summary (one paragraph)

Thailand Boat Festival Edition 2 ran January 15–18, 2026, at Phuket Boat Lagoon Marina — the show's confirmed long-term home. Per Asia-Pacific Boating (January 2026), the event featured 44 boats on water (fully booked) from 24 brands and 72 exhibitors. Seven world and regional premieres were unveiled. The venue switch from Yacht Haven to Boat Lagoon reflects a deepened partnership with Boat Lagoon Yachting (BLY), one of Southeast Asia's largest yacht dealerships.

## Key Details

- **Dates:** January 15–18, 2026 (four days)
- **Venue:** Phuket Boat Lagoon Marina, central Phuket
- **Marina specs:** 180 wet berths, 144 hardstands, max LOA ~36m. Operated by BLY (exclusive Princess/Jeanneau dealer for SE Asia). Per PredictWind
- **Organizer:** M Vision Public Company Limited, in partnership with BLY
- **Attendance:** not published — never state visitor or VIP numbers
- **Exhibitors:** 72 exhibitors, 44 boats on water (fully booked), 24 brands
- **Premieres:** Seven (full list not published)
- **Entry:** Free with registration; VIP passes available
- **Format:** In-water berthing (Dock A–F), on-land displays, lifestyle pavilion, VIP tents
- **Next edition:** TBF 2027 — January 14–17, 2027, Boat Lagoon (per thailandboatfestival.com)
