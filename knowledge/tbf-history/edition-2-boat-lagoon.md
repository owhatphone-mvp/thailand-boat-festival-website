## Summary (one paragraph)

Thailand Boat Festival Edition 2 ran January 15–18, 2026, at Phuket Boat Lagoon Marina — the show's confirmed long-term home. Per Asia-Pacific Boating (January 2026), the event featured 50 yachts from 24 brands, 72 exhibitors, and drew 6,380 attendees — the largest on-land yacht display ever held in Thailand. Seven world and regional premieres were unveiled. The venue switch from Yacht Haven to Boat Lagoon reflects a deepened partnership with Boat Lagoon Yachting (BLY), one of Southeast Asia's largest yacht dealerships.

## Key Details

- **Dates:** January 15–18, 2026 (four days)
- **Venue:** Phuket Boat Lagoon Marina, central Phuket
- **Marina specs:** 180 wet berths, 144 hardstands, max LOA ~36m. Operated by BLY (exclusive Princess/Jeanneau dealer for SE Asia). Per PredictWind
- **Organizer:** Dream Marine Events, in partnership with BLY
- **Attendance:** 6,380 (per Asia-Pacific Boating, January 2026)
- **Exhibitors:** 72 exhibitors, 50 yachts, 24 brands
- **Premieres:** Seven (full list not published)
- **Entry:** Free with registration; VIP passes available
- **Format:** In-water berthing (Dock A–F), on-land displays, lifestyle pavilion, sea trials, sunset cruises, VIP tents
- **Next edition:** TBF 2027 — January 14–17, 2027, Boat Lagoon (per thailandboatfestival.com)

## Context for Sand

- Boat Lagoon is strategic: BLY is the anchor partner. Smaller (180 vs 320 berths) but centrally located (~30 min from airport).
- Mid-January timing aligns with peak season and ahead of Singapore Yachting Festival (April).
- 6,380 attendance is significant for a niche luxury event. TIBS claims 6,000+ (similar scale, different format).
- TBF 2027 dates overlap with TIBS 2027 — handle diplomatically.
- Lifestyle programming (HondaJet, Aston Martin) signals luxury festival ambition, a differentiator in Asia.
