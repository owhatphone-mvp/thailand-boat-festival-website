## Summary (one paragraph)

The first Thailand Boat Festival (TBF) took place November 13–17, 2024, at Phuket Yacht Haven Marina. Per Prestige Online (November 2024), the five-day event drew over 100 exhibitors and displayed approximately 45 boats, gathering "the big names in boating" alongside luxury lifestyle brands. The venue — Phuket's largest marina with 320 berths, closest to the airport — made it logistically attractive for fly-in VIPs from Bangkok, Hong Kong, Singapore, and mainland China.

## Key Details

- **Dates:** November 13–17, 2024 (five days)
- **Venue:** Phuket Yacht Haven Marina, Mai Khao, northern Phuket
- **Marina specs:** 320 berths, vessels up to 100m LOA, 6m+ depth, ~15 min from airport. Per TYHA, features superyacht berths and a full-service yard
- **Organizer:** Dream Marine Events. Per Yacht Style (January 2026), founder Araya launched the company for a premium Thai boat festival
- **Exhibitor count:** 100+ exhibitors, ~45 boat displays (per Prestige Online, November 2024)
- **Format:** In-water displays, on-land displays, lifestyle pavilions, sea trials, sunset champagne cruises, VIP lounges
- **Entry:** Free with pre-registration
- **Timing:** Peak tourism season; just weeks before Phuket King's Cup Regatta

## Context for Sand

- Edition 1 was a proof-of-concept. TBF moved to Boat Lagoon Marina for Edition 2 (January 2026), operated by BLY, a core partner.
- Yacht Haven later became the permanent venue for TIBS (a separate event). Sand should not conflate the two shows.
- The November timing caught early-season visitors. The shift to January for Edition 2 aligns better with peak season and the Asian boat show calendar (before Singapore in April).
- Edition 1 established TBF's identity: curated luxury festival, not a mass-market trade show.

## DO NOT

- Claim Edition 1 was in 2023 — it was November 2024
- Confuse TBF with TIBS (separate event organized by JAND Events)
- State definitive visitor numbers — no confirmed figure published

- Say Yacht Haven is the "permanent" TBF venue — TBF has moved to Boat Lagoon
