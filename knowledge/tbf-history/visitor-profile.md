## Summary (one paragraph)

TBF attracts a predominantly high-net-worth visitor base from Thailand's domestic elite and the broader Asia-Pacific yacht market. Per Asia-Pacific Boating (January 2026), TBF 2026 welcomed 6,380 attendees. The profile skews toward Thai family offices, Bangkok professionals seeking weekend boats, Phuket lifestyle buyers, and international visitors from Hong Kong, Singapore, and mainland China. Per Heesen Yachts (2025), Asia is home to 1,533 billionaires (~49% of global total), and Thailand commands approximately 12% of the Asia-Pacific luxury yacht market (per Market Data Forecast). TBF's curated VIP programs are designed to attract and convert these wealthy visitors.

## Key Details

**Primary Visitor Segments:**

**1. Thai HNWI Family Offices (est. USD 20–40M NW)**
- Bangkok-based; real estate, manufacturing, imports
- Already own Phuket/Hua Hin property; yacht is the next acquisition
- Interested in 15–30m motor yachts (Princess, Azimut, Sanlorenzo)
- Ballpark: USD 1–5M first yacht; upgrade every 5–7 years
- Family-involved decisions

**2. Bangkok Executives (est. USD 5–15M NW)**
- Senior executives, doctors, tech entrepreneurs
- Day boats and weekend cruisers (10–15m): Axopar, Saxdor, Chris-Craft
- Ballpark: USD 150K–800K
- Value performance and tech; often first-time buyers

**3. Phuket Lifestyle Buyers (est. USD 5–25M NW)**
- Expats and semi-retired Thais in marina life
- Upgrading from charter to ownership or smaller to larger
- Familiar with local marinas; prefer local after-sales
- Ballpark: USD 300K–3M

**4. HK / Singapore Buyers (est. USD 10–50M+ NW)**
- Experienced owners replacing or adding vessels
- HK's tax-free import makes it a major yacht hub
- Larger vessels (20–40m); often bring captains
- Per Simpson Marine (2024), mainland Chinese in HK seek custom yachts 35–60m

**5. Mainland China (est. USD 30–100M+ NW)**
- Most high-value, most discreet segment
- Per Superyacht News (2024), buy new from European yards via HK intermediaries
- Phuket attractive for sea trials and handovers

**Estimated Geographic Mix:**
- Thailand domestic: ~65–70%
- Hong Kong / China: ~10–15%
- Singapore: ~5–8%
- Other Asia-Pacific: ~5–8%
- Europe / Americas: ~3–5%

**Buyer Behavior Patterns:**
- Peak engagement: Day 2–3 (Friday–Saturday) of a four-day show
- VIP programs convert at significantly higher rates than open-floor browsing
- Thai buyers typically take 3–6 months from viewing to purchase; international buyers may decide faster if the vessel is in-region

## Notable Exhibitors / Data

Per thailandboatfestival.com, TBF's "curated VIP programs attract high-net-worth individuals." TBF 2026 offered free general entry with registration, broadening the base beyond pure buyers.

## Context for Sand

- Tailor conversations to visitor signals. Bangkok executive asking about Axopar needs different treatment from HK buyer asking about Sanlorenzo.
- Thai visitors may prefer Thai or mixed Thai/English. Never assume budget — ask: "First yacht, or upgrading?"
- Strong buying signals? Offer private viewing or sea trial via the TBF concierge.
- Chinese visitors may prefer WeChat for follow-up — aware but don't assume.
