## Summary

Pre-purchase surveys and classification society oversight are essential pillars of yacht ownership, providing buyers and insurers with verified assessments of a vessel's condition, safety, and regulatory compliance. In Southeast Asia, Phuket and Singapore serve as the primary hubs for qualified marine surveyors, while major classification societies maintain regional presences to support the growing Asian superyacht fleet.

## Key Information

**Pre-Purchase Surveys**

A pre-purchase survey is the most comprehensive condition assessment before acquiring a yacht. Per ACP Surveyors (2025), it covers hull structure, deck and superstructure, machinery and propulsion, electrical systems, plumbing, safety equipment, interior condition, and sea trial performance. The surveyor produces a detailed report with findings categorized by severity (critical defects, recommended repairs, observations) and often includes a valuation. Per YachtWorld (2026), survey costs typically range USD 20-35 per foot of vessel length -- a 60-foot yacht might cost USD 1,200-2,100, while a 100-foot superyacht can run USD 3,000-7,000 or more. The buyer pays for the survey; haul-out costs are shared or buyer-borne.

**Classification Societies**

Classification societies establish and verify technical standards for yacht construction and maintenance. The four major societies are: RINA (Italian, leading class for yachts, strong Mediterranean and Asian presence); Lloyd's Register (UK, oldest classification society, dedicated yacht division); ABS (American Bureau of Shipping, rules addressing strength, stability, safety); and Bureau Veritas (French, structural and reliability assessments). Per Boat International (2025), classification involves plan approval during construction, periodic surveys (annual, intermediate, special), and class certificates required by most insurers and flag states.

**MCA Compliance**

The UK Maritime and Coastguard Agency (MCA) sets the Large Commercial Yacht Code (LYC) and Passenger Yacht Code (PYC) for chartering yachts. Per Superyacht Partners (2025), MCA compliance is mandatory for yachts carrying more than 12 passengers commercially; most charter yachts are limited to 12 guests for regulatory reasons. MCA coding covers stability, lifesaving appliances, fire protection, navigation equipment, and crew qualifications. Though a UK authority, MCA codes are adopted internationally and referenced by many flag states.

**When to Survey**

Key milestones: pre-purchase (before acquisition); annual or biennial condition surveys (required by class and insurers); post-incident (after grounding, collision, fire, or damage); pre-charter (before entering charter programs); and refit supervision (during major repair or modification).

**Surveyors in Phuket and Singapore**

Phuket firms include: Waterline Marine (since 2007, pre-purchase, insurance, maintenance surveys); Andaman Maritime Services (independent surveys for yachts and commercial vessels); Waterborne Experts (pre-purchase condition surveys and consulting); and Group 3A (survey and consultancy across Thailand). Singapore hosts additional surveyors and classification society offices including RINA, Lloyd's Register, and ABS with dedicated yacht teams.

## Asian / Thai Market Context

Thailand does not mandate classification for Thai-flagged private pleasure craft. However, virtually all insurers require class certification or a satisfactory condition survey. For Thai-flagged commercial yachts, the Thai Marine Department imposes its own survey requirements. Foreign-flagged yachts in Thai waters typically carry class from an IACS-member society, simplifying insurance and port state control. Phuket's surveyor concentration reflects its status as Southeast Asia's largest recreational boating hub.

## Talking Points for Sand

- Always recommend a pre-purchase survey regardless of how well-maintained a yacht appears.
- Classification directly affects insurability, resale value, and charter eligibility.
- For buyers considering chartering, flag MCA compliance early.
- Suggest buyers choose a surveyor independent of the broker or seller.
- Phuket offers convenient access to qualified surveyors for Asian-based yacht inspections.

## DO NOT

- Guarantee a clean survey means the yacht is defect-free -- surveys are visual and non-destructive.
- Recommend a single surveyor -- provide a shortlist.
- Downplay classification's importance for insurance.
- Confuse flag state requirements with classification society requirements.
