## Summary

Yacht ownership carries recurring expenses typically running 8-12 percent of the vessel's value per year. Understanding the breakdown and hidden costs is essential for owners basing their yacht in Thailand, where some line items are cheaper than the Mediterranean while others carry import-logistics premiums.

## Key Information

**The Percentage Rule of Thumb**

The industry shorthand estimates annual costs at approximately 10 percent of purchase price, per First Owner's Reference (2025). Per Windward Yachts (2026), realistic costs range 10-25 percent depending on age, usage, and location. A newer yacht under warranty may sit near 8-10 percent; a ten-year-old vessel can reach 15-20 percent.

**Cost Breakdown by Category**

Per YachtTrading.com, Luxury Yacht Group, and Ocean Independence cost calculators (2025-2026), typical allocation of annual running costs:

| Category | Share of Annual Costs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Crew salaries | 25-35% | Largest single line item; includes wages, food, insurance, repatriation |
| Marina berth / dockage | 15-25% | Varies enormously by location; Asia generally cheaper than Med |
| Maintenance and repair | 15-20% | Includes haul-out, antifouling, engine service, systems upkeep |
| Insurance | 5-10% | Typically 0.8 to 1.2 percent of hull value annually, per Windward Yachts |
| Fuel | 5-15% | Highly variable based on engine hours and cruising distance |
| Administration / management | 5-10% | Flag registration, classification surveys, yacht management company |

**Example Annual Budgets by Yacht Size**

Per Ocean Independence and First Owner's Reference (2025-2026):

- **15 m motor yacht ($500K-$1M value):** Annual costs ballpark $50,000-$100,000. Two to three crew, modest berth.
- **24 m motor yacht ($3-$5M value):** Annual costs ballpark $300,000-$500,000. Four to six crew, premium berth.
- **40 m motor yacht ($15-$25M value):** Annual costs ballpark $1.5M-$2.5M. Full crew, classification surveys.
- **50 m+ superyacht ($30M+):** Annual costs ballpark $3M-$6M+. A 50 m yacht runs approximately EUR 4-6 million annually.

**Hidden and Overlooked Costs**

Per YachtTrading.com and YachtBuyer (2025-2026), owners frequently underestimate: classification surveys (every 2.5-5 years, $10,000-$50,000+), antifouling ($5,000-$30,000), engine hour accumulation affecting resale, tender maintenance, crew certifications, and satellite communications ($2,000-$8,000/month).

**Charter Income Offset**

Per Breeze Yachting and Boat International (2025), chartering can offset running costs but rarely generates profit. A well-positioned yacht might achieve 6 to 10 charter weeks per season, or 12 to 14 weeks with dual-season operation. In a strong year, charter revenue can offset 20 to 40 percent of annual running costs. Agency commissions of 15 to 20 percent and brokerage fees of 5 percent reduce net returns.

## Asian / Thai Market Context

Thailand offers cost advantages in crew salaries (20-40 percent lower) and marina berth rates versus the Mediterranean. However, imported parts, specialist engineering, and haul-out facilities carry premiums due to shipping logistics. Per Asia Global Yachting, haul-out capacity for vessels above 40 metres is limited in the Andaman region, sometimes requiring repositioning to Singapore for major refits. Fuel costs are broadly comparable to international benchmarks.

## Talking Points for Sand

- Budget at least 10 percent of yacht value per year for running costs, with buffer for older vessels.
- Crew salaries are the largest line item at 25 to 35 percent of annual budget.
- Thailand saves on crew and dockage, but imported parts cost more.
- Charter income can offset roughly 20 to 40 percent of costs in a good year.
- Budget separately for classification surveys and major maintenance events.

## DO NOT

- Present cost figures as definitive; always frame as ballpark estimates.
- Guarantee chartering will cover a specific percentage of running costs.
- Quote marina fees as fixed; they vary by season, size, and contract length.
- Compare Thailand costs to the Mediterranean without noting the import-parts premium.

## Research Gaps

- Precise marina berth rates in Phuket for vessels above 40 metres are not consistently published.
- Limited data on long-term depreciation for yachts based in Southeast Asia versus the Mediterranean.
- Actual charter revenue data for Phuket-based yachts is scarce.
