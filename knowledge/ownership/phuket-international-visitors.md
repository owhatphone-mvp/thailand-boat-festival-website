## Summary

Phuket is one of Asia's most accessible yachting hubs for international owners, with world-class healthcare, English-speaking marina communities, and multiple long-stay visa pathways. Understanding visa options, healthcare, schooling, and transport is essential whether arriving for a season or basing a yacht year-round.

## Key Information

**Visa Options for Yacht Owners**

Per Siam Legal and ThailandElite.fr (2026):

- **Tourist Visa:** Most Western nationalities get 30-60 days visa-free or a 60-day tourist visa.
- **Thailand Privilege Visa (formerly Elite):** 5-20 year residency. Bronze: 650,000 THB (approximately $18,700) for 5 years; Gold: 5 million THB (approximately $143,000) for 20 years. Includes airport fast-track and multiple re-entries.
- **Retirement Visa (O-A):** Ages 50+, 800,000 THB in Thai bank or 65,000 THB/month income. Annual renewal.
- **LTR Visa:** 10-year, approximately 100,000 THB fee with tax incentives, per HLB Thai (2025).

**Crew Visa Requirements**

Per the Thai Ministry of Foreign Affairs, foreign crew must hold a Non-Immigrant B visa paired with a work permit. The yacht's Thai agent sponsors both (processing: two to four weeks). Enforcement has increased; crew on tourist visas face fines and deportation. The DTV visa is for remote workers and is not valid for yacht crew.

**Healthcare Facilities**

Phuket has several internationally accredited hospitals:

- **Bangkok Hospital Phuket:** BDMS network, JCI accredited, International Patient Services with interpreter support in 16 languages. Primary facility for complex needs.
- **Siriroj International Hospital:** Mid-size, international-standard care near the marina district.
- **Phuket International Hospital:** Routine and emergency care.

All accept international health insurance with cashless billing. Emergency evacuation to Bangkok is available.

**International Schools**

Per Phuket Expat Guide and International Schools Database (2025-2026):

- **BISP:** IB and British curricula, ages 2-18. Fees approximately 420,000-780,000 THB.
- **UWC Thailand:** IB Continuum, ages 3-19 (boarding from 16). Fees approximately 700,000-950,000 THB. CIS accredited.
- **HeadStart:** IB and English National, ages 2-18. Fees approximately 280,000-520,000 THB.

**Transport and Connectivity**

Phuket International Airport (HKT) serves 67 non-stop destinations per AirportInformation.com (2026), including Bangkok, Singapore, Hong Kong, KL, Dubai, and Chinese and Australian cities. Per Phuket News Property (2026), expanded European and South Asian services have been added. The airport is 30 minutes from the marina district.

**Language and Currency**

English is widely spoken in marinas and yacht service companies. Thai is useful for local and government interactions. THB is official; USD are accepted at marinas and yacht services.

**Safety and Infrastructure**

Phuket is among Southeast Asia's safest destinations with low violent crime. Infrastructure includes 4G/5G, high-speed internet, and good roads. A tsunami early warning system is in place with sirens and marked evacuation routes.

## Asian / Thai Market Context

Phuket stands out in Asia for combining marine infrastructure, international services, and affordability. Marina berths and daily expenses are substantially lower than Singapore or Hong Kong. The Thailand Privilege visa offers one of the region's most accessible long-term residency options. JCI-accredited healthcare and international schools on a small island is unusual for a yachting destination outside Europe.

## Talking Points for Sand

- The Thailand Privilege visa is the most hassle-free long-stay option for owners basing a yacht across multiple seasons.
- Bangkok Hospital Phuket offers international-standard care with multilingual support and insurance billing.
- Foreign crew must have Non-Immigrant B visas and work permits; tourist visas are not sufficient.
- Phuket airport has direct flights to major Asian hubs, making crew rotations convenient.
- BISP and UWC Thailand are well-regarded schools for owners relocating families.

## DO NOT

- Quote visa fees or processing times as guaranteed; these change with Thai immigration policy updates.
- Name specific yacht owners or reference their visa arrangements or residency status.
- Suggest that tourist visas are sufficient for crew working on yachts in Thai waters.
- Recommend specific immigration lawyers or agents without verified credentials.

## Research Gaps

- Comparative healthcare cost data between Phuket hospitals and international benchmarks is limited.
