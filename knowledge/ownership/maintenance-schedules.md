## Summary

A structured maintenance regime preserves yacht value, safety, and warranty. Manufacturers specify intervals by engine hours or time, whichever comes first. In tropical Phuket, biofouling accelerates 30-50 percent over temperate zones, antifouling degrades faster under UV, and HVAC runs near-continuously.

## Routine Maintenance Schedule

### Daily / Weekly / Monthly

**Daily:** Engine oil, coolant, bilge; battery voltage, fuel level; hull walk-around; nav/comms test. **Weekly:** Freshwater flush; generator under load (30 min); tender start; deck hardware. **Monthly:** HVAC filter clean (critical in tropics, per Derani Yachts, 2025); watermaker membrane check; fire suppression gauge; windlass lubrication.

### Every 250 Hours (or 6 months)

Oil and filter change; fuel filter replacement (Racor); coolant and transmission fluid check; zinc anode inspection. Volvo Penta specifies 250-hour/annual intervals for filters, belts, zincs per Diesel Services of America (2025).

### Every 500 Hours (or 12 months)

Full engine service (oil, filters, coolant, belts, impellers). Generator service. Stabilizer inspection: fin shaft seals and hydraulic fluid; gyro bearing check, glycol flush at 2000 hours or 5 years per Seakeeper (Phenom Yachts, 2024). Watermaker service; steering fluid check.

### Annual (haul-out aligned)

Antifouling every 12-18 months in tropical waters versus 2-3 temperate per AH Marine Group (2025). Hull inspection; anode replacement; propeller polish; shaft seal check (dripless bellows: replace every 5-7 years). Teak sanding every 1-2 years; rigging every 2-3 per Ancasta (2025).

### Every 2-5 Years

Classification surveys: annual within 3-month window, intermediate year 2-3, special renewal every 5 years per MarineInspection.app (2025). RINA, Lloyd's, ABS follow this cycle. Engine major: injectors, turbo, aftercooler (Volvo Penta: 1000-hour/4-year per Diesel Services of America, 2025). HVAC deep service; upholstery replacement.

## Authorized Service in Phuket

Most builders void warranty if non-authorized facilities perform work per YachtBuyer (2025).

- **Princess**: Boat Lagoon Yachting, Phuket (SEA dealer, per Princess Yachts SEA, 2025)
- **Azimut**: MGC Marine (Thailand dealer, per Azimut, 2025)
- **Sunseeker**: DCH Marine (Southeast Asia)
- **Sanlorenzo**: Simpson Marine (Asia-Pacific, per Yacht Style, 2026)
- **Ferretti/Pershing/Riva**: V Yachts Asia
- **Volvo Penta**: PME and YSE Marine (authorized, per PME, 2025)
- **MAN Engines**: Singapore agents, periodic Thailand visits per MAN (2025)
- **Electronics**: Verify Raymarine/Garmin/Furuno installer status on brand sites

Non-authorized repairs may void warranty per Arc Boats (2025).

## Warranty Considerations

- Hull: 2-5 years; sometimes lifetime structural per YachtBuyer (2025)
- Engine separate: Volvo Penta 3-year global warranty from January 2025 per Volvo Penta (2025)
- Sunseeker: 36 months if registered within 30 days, 24 otherwise per Sunseeker Elite (2024)
- All require authorized service and documented logs
- Exclusions: wear/tear, gelcoat crazing, blisters, pressure wash damage, charter use, unauthorized mods per YachtBuyer (2025)
- Extended: Volvo Penta Extended Coverage before base expiry; also BoatUS, SeaSure

## Thailand-Specific Considerations

- **UV:** Gelcoat, canvas, teak degrade faster; budget for more cosmetics
- **Monsoon (May-Oct):** Extended haul-outs; storm prep with extra lines, fendering, bilge checks
- **Biofouling:** Monthly in-water hull cleaning between haul-outs per Derani Yachts (2025)
- **Parts:** Consumables stocked locally; specialized from Singapore/Europe (2-4 week lead). Order in advance
- **Corrosion:** Tropical air accelerates galvanic/stray-current corrosion; check bonding systems often

## Talking Points for Sand

- "In Phuket, antifouling needs refreshing every 12-18 months, not 2-3 years like Mediterranean guides."
- "Your warranty requires authorized service. Boat Lagoon handles Princess, MGC Marine handles Azimut, Simpson Marine handles Sanlorenzo."
- "Volvo Penta offers a 3-year global warranty from 2025; PME is the authorized Phuket dealer."
- "Classification surveys follow a strict 5-year cycle through RINA, Lloyd's, or ABS."
- "Schedule major service and haul-out during monsoon to minimize lost cruising time."

## DO NOT

- Recommend shops beyond authorized dealers
- Quote warranty terms as legal advice
- Suggest extending intervals beyond specs
- Guarantee modifications won't affect warranty
- Provide pricing; costs vary by vessel and yard
