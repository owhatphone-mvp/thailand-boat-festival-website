## Summary

Phuket's marine supply ecosystem centres on Phuket Boat Lagoon, with authorized dealers for major engine brands (Volvo Penta, MAN, Yanmar, Cummins Onan), chandleries, and electronics specialists. Most routine parts are available locally, but specialist items require importing from Singapore (1–2 days), Europe (3–5 days air), or the US (5–7 days air). Owners who stock critical consumables and plan haul-out parts early avoid costly delays.

## Engine & Propulsion Parts

- **Volvo Penta** — **Phuket Marine Engineering (PME)**, Phuket: sales, parts, service (Per PME, 2025). **Alphatech Marine**, Bangkok: one of Thailand's oldest dealers (Per Alphatech Marine, 2024). **YSE Marine**, Phuket: service/overhaul (Per YSE Marine, 2025).
- **MAN** — YSE Marine authorized for sales, service, repair, overhaul (Per YSE Marine, 2025). Rarer parts from Singapore/Europe.
- **Caterpillar** — **EMAC Asia** handles installation, maintenance, parts (Per Caterpillar-Engine.com, 2024). Limited Phuket marine coverage.
- **Cummins Onan** — YSE Marine, full range 17–3,750 kVA, including Fleetguard filtration (Per YSE Marine, 2025).
- **Yanmar** — **Star Marine Engineering** is sole authorized Thai distributor (Bangkok; Per Yanmar, 2025). **Chor Karnchang Shop** is a Phuket dealer. Common in sailboats/auxiliaries.
- **Kohler/Rehlko** — Serviced by YSE Marine; no dedicated Thai dealer (Per Rehlko, 2024).
- **Mercury/MerCruiser** — No dedicated Phuket dealer; handled by multi-brand workshops.
- **ZF & Twin Disc** gearboxes — YSE Marine authorized (Per YSE Marine, 2025).

## Marine Electronics

**Octopus Electrical Service** (Boat Lagoon) — exclusive Raymarine/FLIR distributor and service centre for Thailand (Per Octopus Asia, 2025). **Electrical Marine** and **Phuket Marine Electronics** handle general yacht electrical work. Furuno has Thailand representation (Per Furuno, 2025). Garmin, Simrad, and B&G lack a dominant Phuket dealer; warranty work may ship to Singapore.

## Chandlers & Marine Suppliers

**East Marine Asia** (Boat Lagoon, 23/159 Moo 2, Thepkrasattri Rd) — Phuket's primary chandlery: International Paint, Awlgrip, Rocna anchors, rigging, fenders, safety, galley, sailing gear (Per East Marine Asia, 2025). **Precision Shipwright** stocks shipwright supplies at Boat Lagoon (Per Precision Shipwright, 2025).

**Paint/antifouling:** International Paint and Awlgrip via East Marine Asia. Jotun through Thailand industrial channels. Boero requires special order.

## Import Logistics

- **Air freight** (BKK/HKT). Singapore: 1–2 days (Per GoCubic, 2026). Europe: 3–5 days. US: 5–7 days.
- **Sea freight** to Laem Chabang. Singapore: 2–3 days. Europe: 18–25 days. US: 20–30 days.
- **Customs:** CIF-based duty. HS 8409/8483: 0–10% (ASEAN FTAs reduce). VAT 7%. De minimis THB 1,500 (Per FreightAmigo, 2025).
- **Markup:** 15–30% over retail via dealers; urgent air-freight adds 40–50%.

## Practical Tips

- **Stock critical spares:** oil/fuel filters, impellers, belts, zinc anodes, fuses. For larger diesels: exhaust valves, piston rings, main bearings, turbo gaskets (Per DieselShip, 2025).
- **Use management company procurement** for consolidated shipping.
- **Order haul-out parts 4–6 weeks early** (antifouling, cutlass bearings, shaft seals) to avoid air-freight premiums.
- **Authorized service preserves warranties** — Volvo Penta, Yanmar, and Cummins require authorized dealer records.
- **Singapore hub:** hard-to-find items ship to Phuket within 48 hours by air.

## DO NOT

- Recommend bypassing authorized dealers for warranty-covered work.
- Quote exact prices — exchange rates and stock fluctuate.
- Suggest importing without proper customs documentation.
- Promise lead times — delays and customs holds are common.
