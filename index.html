The venue

Land and water in one marina

Boat Lagoon is a marina village on Phuket's east coast, with docks, a waterfront promenade, restaurants, shops and a resort hotel in one place. For four days all of it becomes the festival.หมู่บ้านมารีน่าฝั่งตะวันออกของภูเก็ต มีท่าเรือ ทางเดินริมน้ำ ร้านอาหาร ร้านค้า และรีสอร์ต ครบในที่เดียว

Captain's notes

Tides, channel & move-in

Boat Lagoon is reached through a marked channel that is shallow at low water. Plan transits on the rising tide. Inside, the basin holds at least 4 m at any tide.ร่องน้ำเข้า Boat Lagoon ตื้นช่วงน้ำลง ควรเข้าช่วงน้ำขึ้น ภายในแอ่งจอดลึกอย่างน้อย 4 เมตรทุกระดับน้ำ

Good to know

Questions, answered

The short answers visitors, buyers and exhibitors ask most.คำถามที่พบบ่อย สำหรับผู้เข้าชม ผู้ซื้อ และผู้ออกงาน · อ่านภาษาไทย

When and where is Thailand Boat Festival 2027?

14–17 January 2027, daily 14:00–21:00, at Boat Lagoon Marina, Phuket: 22/1 Moo 2, Thepkrasattri Rd, Koh Kaew. The marina is 9 km from Phuket Town and about 20 minutes by car from Phuket International Airport.

How much is entry?

Register for the Early Bird 4-Day Pass by 30 November 2026 and entry is complimentary for all four days (worth 400 THB). The VIP Pass is 3,900 THB including VAT until 31 December 2026 (regular 5,500 THB). Get your Early Bird Pass.

What is there to see?

50+ yachts in the water across five docks, from day boats to yachts of about 29 m, and 100+ booths on land with supercars and luxury cars, coastal real estate, private aviation, marine equipment and lifestyle brands. The 2027 line-up is announced as brands confirm.

Can I see the yachts up close?

Yes. Visitors walk the floating pontoons and see each yacht up close as it floats. VIP Pass holders can book yacht viewings by appointment.

How do I exhibit a boat or take a booth?

Berths are available across Docks C, E, F, G and MC for yachts up to about 29 m. Land booths come in 3 × 3 m, 6 × 6 m and 10 × 4 m, and the White House hall is available as a full buyout. 2026 exhibitors choose their berths first, with Early Bird rates on a 50% deposit by 6 November 2026. Email info@thailandboatfestival.com for the berth plan, land plan and rates.

Is there parking, and where can I stay?

There is on-site parking for all guests, and a resort hotel inside the marina. Arriving by sea, use the Boat Lagoon channel on the rising tide and call the Harbour Master on VHF 71.
